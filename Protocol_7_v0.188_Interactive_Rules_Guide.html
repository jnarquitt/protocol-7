Protocol Dice

The dice in your hand describe why the Vector can accomplish the action. The Skill die shows training. Three Ability dice show the parts of the character involved. Gear, VAMs, Advantage and Mastery enter only through their own named rules.

Roll the complete pool together. Faces 1–3 are blank. Every face of 4+ contributes its visible value.

Vitality Ritual

Roll STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS and CHA once each. The app may roll all six at once; physical dice work identically. If at least one die shows 4+, starting HP is 18 + the highest face. If all six are blank, starting HP is 28.

Current Vitality experiment.

If multiple Abilities tie for the highest qualifying result, choose one tied Ability as Vitality Origin.

Vitality Spark — once per session: after a Skill roll whose three listed Ability dice include your Origin, if the pool contains a natural 1, reroll one die showing 1 and keep the new result.

If all six Vitality dice are blank, you instead gain Perfect Equilibrium — once per session: after any Skill roll containing a natural 1, reroll one die showing 1 and keep the new result.

These are active playtest experiments, not locked rules.

Build a Roll

Normal pool: one Skill die + the three Ability dice listed for that Skill. Repeated Ability references are intentional.

  • One primary relevant Gear die when applicable.
  • A VAM die only when its rule explicitly grants one.
  • Advantage adds one d6.
  • Disadvantage removes the smallest Ability die; choose if tied.
  • A Mastery die only when the Skill is Mastered and explicit Mastery access is active.

Advantage and Disadvantage reasons cancel one-for-one. Each side caps at one effective instance. Gear is never simply Advantage.

Combat & Actions

3 AP per turn.
Normal Attack: 1 AP.
Movement: 30 feet per AP — 30/60/90 feet for 1/2/3 AP. There is no universal Run action or ×2 multiplier.

Combat uses active defense. Attacker and defender build their legal pools and roll. If attacker total does not exceed defense, no normal damage is inflicted. If attacker wins, normal damage equals winning margin. Relevant armor normally adds its Gear die to defense and does not subtract damage again.

Reaction

You normally have one Reaction between turns. It is not a free fourth AP. Unspent AP from your turn remains banked for the Reaction. Spend banked AP first. Only if none remains may you borrow at most 1 AP from your next turn. Borrowed AP reduces that next turn; unused banked AP expires when the next turn begins.

VAMs, BAR, Gear & Conditions

BAR is capacity, not fuel. Loading into unused BAR costs 0 AP. During active play, replacing/unloading loaded VAMs as a field reconfiguration costs 1 AP for the whole swap.

The current catalog has 84 VAMs across Combat; Defense & Mobility; Recon & Investigation; Social & Cover; Technical & Medical; Command & Temporal. VAM dice remain VAM dice. A VAM that removes Disadvantage removes a named reason; it does not silently grant Advantage.

Normally one primary relevant Gear die applies. Mission loadouts use equipment authorized or reasonably available for the assignment; the GM adjudicates unusual or excessive selections while the Gear economy continues to be playtested.

Current conditions: Prone, Grappled, Suppressed, Stunned, Disoriented, Restrained. Conditions use named rules language rather than private penalty dice. Stunned removes Reaction and increases the AP cost of the first action next turn by 1.

Level 2 Edges

  • Driven: once/session, after rolling reroll one die; keep new result.
  • Inspired: once/session, gain Advantage before a roll.
  • Prepared: once/session, declare reasonable ordinary prior preparation.
  • Connected: once/session, establish a plausible useful contact, introduction or ordinary social access.
  • Durable: persistent +5 maximum HP.

Mastering a Skill does not itself add a die. A Mastery die also requires explicit Mastery access. When legal, it equals the current Skill die; only one Mastery die applies.

Historical Operations

Carrier and Exposure are competing objectives. Carrier states: Fragile → Established → Durable. Outcomes: Clean Success, Exposed Success, Contained Failure, Exposed Failure.

Mission rhythm: Briefing → Insertion → Establish Cover → Investigate → Identify Leverage → Intervene → Establish Carrier → Contain Exposure → Extract → Historical Verification.

Living Playtest

v0.188 is deliberately mutable. Play it, challenge it, and report what feels wrong. A rule being printed here does not make it locked. The important guardrail is that changes are deliberate and older discarded mechanics do not silently return.

Probability-sensitive experiments receive math/simulation before use. Matched Blanks / Dice Tricks and a universal critical subsystem remain outside the current core unless deliberately reintroduced for testing.
