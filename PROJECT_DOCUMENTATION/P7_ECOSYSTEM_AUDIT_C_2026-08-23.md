## FROZEN-MECHANIC CHECK

The campaign continues to assume the v0.187 playtest baseline, including:

- fixed 18 starting HP;
- advancement HP based on half the maximum Strength die;
- three Action Points;
- repeatable normal movement rather than a universal Run action;
- Protocol Dice scoring where 1–3 are blank and 4+ contribute face value;
- VAM/BAR loadout management;
- Carrier and Exposure campaign structures.

M01–M06 do not require a change to these rules to function.

The intended campaign test ladder is now explicit:

- M01: Level 1
- M02: Level 2

- M04: Level 4

The design target is that Protocol 7 Level 1→6 produces approximately the campaign-growth sensation of Level 1→12 in a typical d20 campaign without copying d20 numerical scaling.

This is NOT yet certified. The dedicated Advancement & Power-Curve Audit remains required.

## CAMPAIGN MODE CHECK

The six missions are intentionally differentiated, but the first-pass review identifies a risk of repeated investigative revelation structure. The post-path Style & Balance Audit must measure actual table-time emphasis rather than mission labels.

Desired dimensions to measure across the whole path:

- action/combat;
- investigation;
- exploration;
- social/ethical negotiation;
- survival/disaster pressure;
- technical/problem-solving play;
- Carrier/Exposure decisions;
- historical orientation;
- character mechanical spotlight.

M05 and M06 deliberately increase exploration, rescue, physical crisis, and simultaneous objectives to counterbalance the investigation-heavy middle of the path. The audit must verify that this is sufficient.

## GM COHERENCE CHECK

The later mission revisions establish a stronger standard than the earliest material:

- two-minute whole-adventure explanation;
- GM truth stated plainly;
- false objective vs. true Carrier;
- revelation ladder;
- three-door essential clues;
- fail-forward guidance;
- named NPC motivations;
- troubleshooting by player style;
- substantial read-aloud where the era is unfamiliar;
- explicit historical boundaries.

The GM Quick-Review/Coherence Audit must now bring all six missions to a consistent version of that standard.

## HISTORICAL-RESPECT CHECK

Current design rule remains sound: Vectors operate adjacent to real history and must not steal real historical achievements. This is especially important for M02 and M06. M06 explicitly forbids the Vectors from teaching prehistoric communities civilization, monumental construction, agriculture, religion, or other capabilities those communities possessed themselves.

A dedicated historical-respect audit remains required before release candidate.

## MYSTERY / PAYOFF CHECK

The seven-stroke mystery now has a complete first-pass arc:

1. M01 — unexplained mark associated with a repaired Carrier;
2. M02 — same mark across independently created copies;
3. M03 — evidence the phenomenon can precede the Vectors' immediate intervention;
4. M04 — relationship between independent evidence and continuity;
5. M05 — pattern increasingly reads as relationships among continuity nodes;
6. M06 — reveal: the seven strokes are a routing diagram; six human continuity routes plus a seventh temporal route.

The payoff works conceptually. The Continuity/Foreshadowing Audit must determine whether players receive enough intermediate evidence to infer possibilities without solving the finale too early.
