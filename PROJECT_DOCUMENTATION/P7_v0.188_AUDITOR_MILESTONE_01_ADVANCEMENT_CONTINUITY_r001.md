- Wave 5 is **not** current validation of v0.188 HP progression.
- HP authority for the current candidate must come from the later Vitality work and consolidated Advancement Candidate Specification.

**Verdict:** DOCUMENTARY CONTINUITY WARNING. Do not delete or rewrite Wave 5; mark it superseded for HP when building the final authority map.
