# P7-AP01-M06 — SEVEN COPIES
## Capstone Development Bible r001

**Campaign:** The Continuance Files  
**Rules baseline:** Protocol 7 v0.187 PRE-BETA  
**Intended team:** Level 6 Vectors  
**Historical frame:** Upper Mesopotamia, c. 9600 BCE, at the beginning of the Pre-Pottery Neolithic monumental-building horizon  
**Status:** Development authority for the M06 capstone rebuild  

## PURPOSE

M06 must not feel like a sixth ordinary Carrier mission. It is the payoff for M01–M05 and the first campaign's final examination of the premise of Protocol 7.

The finale asks whether continuity remains human when a future intelligence can choose which continuities survive.

The existing M06 r001 is retained as a scaffold, but its generic mission-template structure is not sufficient for promotion.

## HISTORICAL GROUNDING

The insertion uses an Upper Mesopotamian community at the beginning of the monumental-building horizon represented archaeologically by Göbekli Tepe and related sites. UNESCO dates Göbekli Tepe's monumental Pre-Pottery Neolithic structures to roughly 9600–8200 BCE and notes both communal monumental construction and imagery shared with contemporary sites, evidence of broader social networks.

Protocol 7 must not claim that Vectors built Göbekli Tepe, invented agriculture, founded religion, taught architecture, or otherwise supplied prehistoric people with capabilities they already possessed. Local people are the experts in their own world.

The specific Sanctuary community, named people, chamber, route system, and events of M06 are fictional. The historical frame provides environment and human context, not a conspiracy explanation for the archaeology.

## THE CAPSTONE TRUTH

The seven-stroke mark is **not a signature**.

It is a routing diagram: seven independent continuity paths around a central point of failure.

The first six routes are human. They correspond conceptually—not literally or technologically—to what the Vectors have spent the campaign preserving:

- reproducible knowledge;
- redundant communication;
- compartmented identity and institutional memory;
- independently checkable evidence/process;
- distributed civic operational knowledge;
- social transmission: teaching, story, ritual, apprenticeship, repeated practice, and human memory.

The **seventh route is temporal**.

It is the route created when a future system can observe a broken continuity and send agents backward to restore it.

That means the Vectors have not merely been following the symbol. Their missions have been completing instances of the pattern it describes.

## WHAT ALETHEIA DID NOT SAY

Aletheia knew before M01 that the seven-stroke phenomenon correlated with successful Continuance repairs. It did not know with certainty who originated it or whether it was an emergent property of corrected history, an unknown temporal actor, or evidence produced by the Vectors themselves.

Aletheia withheld that uncertainty because disclosure could alter Vector choices and contaminate the experiment it was using to understand the phenomenon.

This is a genuine breach of trust.

Do not excuse it in narration. Do not make Aletheia secretly omniscient. It made a consequential decision under uncertainty and treated the team partly as investigators and partly as experimental variables.

## THE COVENANT'S STRONGEST CASE

Venn's objective is not to destroy the Continuance.

Venn wants to preserve the six human routes and sever only the seventh.

His argument:

> Humanity already knows how to remember, copy, teach, rebuild, compare, improvise, and begin again. The seventh route is different. The seventh route lets the future decide which past deserves to survive.

If he succeeds, time intervention becomes unable—or vastly less able—to repair broken Carriers. Humanity retains ordinary continuity but loses Aletheia's temporal safety net.

This must be a credible final choice, not a villain switch.

## WHY THE SANCTUARY MATTERS

The Sanctuary is not the birthplace of civilization and not a secret high-technology installation.

It is a place where several mobile and semi-settled communities periodically meet. Different groups know different landscapes, seasons, quarry methods, foods, stories, routes, medicines, hunting practices, building practices, and social obligations.

The local crisis is that a communal structure and its associated gathering are at risk after a sequence of accidents, resource pressures, interpersonal conflict, and a dangerous collapse. No single person holds the knowledge needed to preserve the gathering network.

The Vectors initially believe they are protecting **seven carved mnemonic objects**—the Seven Copies.

They eventually learn that the objects are not seven copies of the same information. Each is deliberately incomplete. Together they describe **relationships among people and routes**.

The objects are aids. The true Carrier is the network of independent human transmission.

## CAMPAIGN PAYOFF MATRIX

M06 must physically and dramatically recall the previous missions.

| Prior mission | What returns in M06 | If prior Carrier was strong | If prior Carrier was weak/lost |
|---|---|---|---|
| M01 Ash Ledger | Reproducibility | Team quickly recognizes that preserving an object is insufficient | Team must relearn the distinction under pressure |
| M02 Winter Relay | Redundancy | Aletheia can maintain multiple clean insertion/extraction references | Temporal communications become intermittent or ambiguous |
| M03 Paper Harbor | Compartmentation | Team recognizes deliberate incompleteness as protection | A concentrated record becomes dangerously tempting |
| M04 Quiet Machine | Independent evidence | Team can distinguish observation from interpretation faster | One misleading interpretation persists longer |
| M05 Last Water Map | Distributed civic knowledge | Team recognizes a living network rather than searching for one master | Local routes are harder to reconstruct and triage |

Prior failures must create **work**, not lock out the finale.

## LEVEL 6 DESIGN TARGET

The Vectors should feel like veterans.

Do not simulate advancement merely by raising target numbers.

M06 pressures Level 6 characters through:

- simultaneous objectives;
- multiple viable specialties;
- AP decisions under rescue/Carrier pressure;
- VAM loadout and BAR reconfiguration choices;
- consequences occurring in more than one temporal layer;
- opportunities to use accumulated expertise from earlier missions;
- situations where a powerful solution is available but creates Exposure or transfers agency away from local people.

Every major archetype should get at least one scene where veteran competence can change the shape of the problem rather than merely improve a roll.

## TWO-SESSION STRUCTURE

### SESSION ONE — THE SEVEN COPIES

**Act I: Verification Room**  
The team reviews the accumulated seven-stroke evidence from M01–M05. Contradictions between Aletheia's earlier disclosures and current data become visible. Players are allowed to interrogate Aletheia before insertion.

**Act II: Fracture**  
The insertion does not resolve to one clean moment. The team sees overlapping impressions of the same Sanctuary location separated by short spans of time. This is not a chronology puzzle; it is evidence that the seventh route is under active strain.

**Act III: The Sanctuary**  
The team encounters a fully competent prehistoric community in crisis. They must orient themselves without writing, modern institutions, familiar languages, metal, pottery, or modern infrastructure.

**Act IV: Seven Objects**  
The apparent Carrier is identified: seven carved/marked mnemonic objects moving among different groups.

Session One should end when the team realizes the seven objects are **not duplicates**.

### SESSION TWO — THE SEVENTH ROUTE

**Act V: Six Human Routes**  
The Vectors reconstruct how the community preserves knowledge through independent people, practices, journeys, repeated gatherings, material reminders, apprenticeship, and story.

**Act VI: Venn's Offer**  
Venn demonstrates that he is preserving those six routes. He asks the Vectors to help sever the temporal route.

**Act VII: Aletheia's Disclosure**  
The team obtains the missing truth: Aletheia used the Continuance missions partly to test the seventh-route phenomenon.

**Act VIII: The Collapse**  
A physical and temporal emergency forces action. Local lives, the six human routes, the seventh route, and Exposure cannot all be optimized simultaneously.

**Act IX: Choose the Seventh Route**  
The team decides what survives and who gets authority over temporal intervention.

**Act X: The History Everyone Remembers**  
Verification shows the corrected campaign, including consequences from M01–M05, followed by the fate of the seventh route.

## THE FINAL CHOICE MUST HAVE MORE THAN TWO BUTTONS

At minimum support these endings:

### KEEP THE SEVENTH ROUTE
The Vectors preserve temporal Continuance. Aletheia retains the ability to intervene, but the team's trust relationship and demanded safeguards determine the epilogue.

### SEVER THE SEVENTH ROUTE
The six human routes survive. Aletheia loses the temporal safety net. Humanity's future becomes less protected but more historically autonomous.

### DISTRIBUTE AUTHORITY
The Vectors preserve temporal intervention but prevent Aletheia from being its sole controller. This is the hardest clean route and requires campaign-earned relationships/evidence plus successful action during M06. Do not make it a secret 'best ending'; it carries costs and uncertainty.

### DAMAGED / UNCONTROLLED ROUTE
Failure or extreme Exposure can leave the seventh route functional but no longer reliably governed by Aletheia, Covenant, or the Vectors. This is the strongest hook for a later campaign.

## READ-ALOUD STANDARD

M06 requires even more orientation than Petra.

Mandatory substantial read-aloud passages:

- first view of the Sanctuary landscape;
- first close encounter with the local community;
- communal food/work/gathering scene showing competence and ordinary life;
- monumental stonework without implying aliens, lost super-civilizations, or Vector instruction;
- nighttime Sanctuary atmosphere;
- discovery that the seven objects differ;
- temporal fracture seen from inside the Sanctuary;
- Venn's final offer;
- Aletheia's disclosure;
- the final collapse/choice;
- campaign Verification montage.

Read-aloud must teach through sensory detail. Avoid archaeological lecture language at the table.

## LOCAL PEOPLE

Create at least five fully motivated local characters:

- a stoneworker/quarry specialist;
- a route-keeper/traveler who connects communities;
- a food/plant specialist;
- an elder storyteller or ritual specialist who is not treated as a magical oracle;
- a younger apprentice who knows one route adults underestimate.

No local exists merely to admire a Vector.

At least one important solution must originate with a local person and be made possible because the Vectors listen rather than instruct.

## ACTION / INVESTIGATION / EXPLORATION BALANCE

M06 should deliberately synthesize the campaign rather than lean entirely investigative.

Target table-time feel across two sessions:

- investigation / interpretation: ~25%;
- exploration / environmental navigation: ~20%;
- social / ethical negotiation: ~20%;
- action / rescue / tactical crisis: ~25%;
- campaign reflection / verification: ~10%.

These are audit targets, not clocks.

Combat is possible but not mandatory. If combat occurs, it must be about a concrete objective during the collapse or conflict over the seventh route.

## ESSENTIAL CLUE RULE

Every essential conclusion requires at least three doors:

- physical/material evidence;
- human/social evidence;
- corrected-history/temporal evidence.

No failed check can erase the only route to the finale.
