# Protocol 7 v0.188 — Provisional Rebuilt VAM Catalog r001

**Status:** SIMULATION CATALOG — not public canon

The complete r001 catalog contains **84 VAM design slots: 14 per functional family**, built around the v0.188 authorization/BAR/Mastery architecture.

## Distribution

Each family receives:
- 4 Level 1 Standard VAMs
- 3 Level 2 Advanced I VAMs
- 2 Level 3 High-Integration VAMs
- 2 Level 4 Advanced II / Skill Mastery VAMs
- 2 Level 5 Elite VAMs
- 1 Level 6 Continuance VAM

Total distribution: L1 24; L2 18; L3 12; L4 12; L5 12; L6 6.

## Level 4 Mastery systems

The provisional catalog includes two veteran/Mastery directions in each family:

- Combat: **Predictive Ballistics Matrix (8 BAR)**; **Close-Combat Forecast (7 BAR)**
- Defense & Mobility: **Kinetic Mastery Suite (8 BAR)**; **Evasion Forecast (8 BAR)**
- Recon & Investigation: **Forensic Synthesis Matrix (8 BAR)**; **Predictive Search Model (7 BAR)**
- Social & Cover: **Behavioral Mastery Model (8 BAR)**; **Linguistic Immersion Matrix (8 BAR)**
- Technical & Medical: **Technical Mastery Kernel (8 BAR)**; **Clinical Mastery Kernel (8 BAR)**
- Command & Temporal: **Command Mastery Mesh (8 BAR)**; **Temporal Analysis Mastery (9 BAR)**

Each requires the corresponding Mastered Skill and only adds the Skill Mastery Die when its bounded condition applies. No entry grants a routine seventh die.

## Level 5–6 direction

Elite VAMs emphasize team interaction, interruption, multi-problem handling, evidence synthesis, identity continuity, group influence, adaptive fabrication, trauma orchestration, and distributed command rather than simple larger bonus dice.

The six Level 6 capstone concepts are:
- **Combat Synthesis — 11 BAR**
- **Survival Envelope — 11 BAR**
- **Continuity Lens — 11 BAR**
- **Cultural Synthesis — 11 BAR**
- **Restoration Synthesis — 11 BAR**

These create strategic choices and Mastery interactions without automatically solving missions.

## BAR grammar

The catalog uses the full BAR range conceptually:
- 1–2: evergreen/narrow utility
- 3–4: specialist capability
- 5–6: strong scene capability
- 7–9: loadout-defining veteran capability and common Mastery access
- 10–12: exceptional/capstone systems

Authorization level and BAR cost remain independent.

## Simulation requirement

Before this catalog becomes player-facing, rebuild all Level 1 presets to legal 16-BAR packages and run persistent Level 1–6 simulations measuring BAR utilization, swaps, Mastery frequency, six-die pool frequency, old-VAM retention, non-Mastery viability, specialist/generalist balance, and peer-enemy Mastery.

The detailed individual rule text is the next working layer; this r001 establishes the complete progression matrix and named veteran/capstone architecture for that build.
