# Protocol 7 v0.188 — Advancement Candidate Specification r001

**Status:** GREEN CANDIDATE — synchronize before player-facing promotion

This document consolidates the advancement architecture that survived simulation Waves 1–5. It supersedes earlier v0.188 working advancement curves where they conflict, but does not itself promote v0.188 to public playtest canon until synchronized products are rebuilt and audited.

## Level 1 — Vector
- Ability array: d8, d8, d6, d6, d4, d4.
- 20 Skill Ranks.
- 18 maximum HP.
- 16 BAR.
- Standard VAM authorization.
- 3 AP and 1 Reaction; these do not increase through Level advancement.

## Every later Level
When gaining a Level, resolve advancement in this order:

1. **HP:** increase maximum HP by half the maximum value of the Vector's current STR die: d4 +2, d6 +3, d8 +4, d10 +5, d12 +6.
2. **Skills:** gain +4 Skill Ranks.
3. **BAR/Authorization:** increase BAR to the new Level's capacity and gain that authorization band.
4. **Level feature:** resolve Edge, Ability growth, or Mastery as listed below.
5. Choose/reconfigure newly legal VAMs.

An Ability increase at the same Level never changes that Level's already-resolved HP award and never recalculates earlier HP.

## Level 2 — Identity
- 18 BAR.
- Advanced I VAM authorization.
- Choose one **Edge**.

Candidate Edges:

**Driven:** once per session after seeing your roll but before outcome narration, reroll one die you rolled. Keep the new result.

**Inspired:** once per session after seeing the initial dice, use d4 as the roll's ordinary bonus die. It obeys the ordinary bonus-die slot and never stacks into a routine seventh die.

**Prepared:** once per session establish one small plausible prior preparation, ordinary resource, or readiness detail consistent with the Vector's access and established fiction.

**Connected:** once per session invoke a plausible useful contact, relationship, introduction, or communication channel. It creates access/opportunity, not automatic cooperation or success.

**Durable:** instead of a renewable session ability, permanently gain +5 maximum HP.

Edges impose no class, Path, Skill, VAM, or Mastery restrictions and may be renamed/reskinned by the player.

## Level 3 — Integration
- 21 BAR.
- High Integration VAM authorization.
- Increase one Ability die by one step, subject to the final Engine Ability ceiling.

## Level 4 — Mastery
- 24 BAR.
- Advanced II VAM authorization.
- Choose one Skill as **Mastered**.
- Mastery-capable VAM authorization begins.

A Mastered Skill has a Skill Mastery Die equal to its current Skill die. The die is dormant unless an appropriate Mastery-capable VAM permits access. When accessed, it enters the pool as the explicit veteran die source beyond the ordinary bonus-die slot.

## Level 5 — Elite
- 27 BAR.
- Elite VAM authorization.
- Increase one Ability die by one step, subject to the final Engine Ability ceiling.

## Level 6 — Continuance
- 30 BAR.
- Continuance VAM authorization.
- Choose a second Skill as Mastered.

Only the Mastery Die belonging to the Skill actually being rolled may enter a roll. Two Mastered Skills never create two Mastery dice on one roll.

## Dice-source rule

A roll is built from:

**Core Pool** — Skill die plus the action's Ability dice under the canonical Engine rule.

**Ordinary Bonus Die** — at most one die from ordinary Feature/VAM, Assist, gear, or situational sources. If several apply, use the one legal source the player chooses or the rule specifically directs.

**Mastery Die** — one Skill Mastery Die when the Skill is Mastered and a legal access mechanism permits it.

The intended common veteran ceiling is therefore Core Pool + one ordinary Bonus Die + one Mastery Die. Seven-plus dice require explicit exceptional text and are not normal optimization.

## No-flat-modifier principle

Protocol Dice bonuses should not be expressed as +1, +2, +3, etc. to rolls, damage, defenses, or similar outcomes.

Use dice, die manipulation, Advantage/Disadvantage, AP/timing, Reactions, movement, conditions, permissions, effect dice, or another bounded dice-facing mechanic.

A fixed change to a resource maximum such as Durable's +5 maximum HP is not a roll modifier and is permitted.

Authorization and BAR cost are independent. High-Level authorization can unlock inexpensive specialist VAMs; expensive VAMs are not automatically high-Level simply because of cost.

## Advancement cadence

- L1: establish the Vector.
- L2: declare a small identity Edge.
- L3: intrinsic Ability growth.
- L4: first Skill Mastery and controlled six-die access.
- L5: second intrinsic Ability growth.
- L6: broaden Mastery and gain Continuance authorization.

This cadence is designed so that Level 4 mirrors the qualitative midpoint jump expected from a longer d20-style campaign while Levels 5–6 expand sophistication rather than beginning uncontrolled dice inflation.
