# Protocol 7 v0.188 — Rebuilt VAM Catalog Final Candidate r001

**Status:** GREEN CANDIDATE — synchronize before public promotion

This document converts the 84-slot v0.188 VAM progression matrix into full candidate rules. Authorization level and BAR cost are independent. VAMs never create routine seven-plus-die pools unless explicitly stated. Where a VAM grants a die, it uses the ordinary Bonus Die slot unless the text says it grants access to a Skill Mastery Die.

## Catalog structure

- 84 VAMs total.
- 14 per functional family.
- Per family: 4 L1 Standard, 3 L2 Advanced I, 2 L3 High Integration, 2 L4 Advanced II/Mastery, 2 L5 Elite, 1 L6 Continuance.
- L1 24; L2 18; L3 12; L4 12; L5 12; L6 6.

# COMBAT

## Level 1 — Standard

**Fast Draw — 1 BAR.** Once per round, ready or stow one accessible one-handed weapon for 0 AP.

**Threat Focus — 2 BAR.** Choose one visible threat you can track. Gain d4 as the ordinary Bonus Die on the first appropriate defense against that threat before your next turn.

**Snap Shot — 3 BAR.** After taking Aim, your next trained ranged attack this turn costs 0 AP instead of its normal AP cost. Once per turn.

**Control Hold — 3 BAR.** On a successful Unarmed/Grappling attack with margin 4+, you may forgo damage to establish a Grapple or comparable close-control state.

## Level 2 — Advanced I

**Overwatch — 4 BAR.** Spend 1 AP while guarding a visible lane or approach. Until your next turn, your Reaction may make one eligible ranged attack against a target that crosses or acts through that lane.

**Suppressive Fire — 5 BAR.** Spend 2 AP with an appropriate automatic weapon. Make the attack against the target's normal defense; on success, inflict Suppressed instead of normal damage unless the weapon/rule explicitly allows both.

**Disarm Protocol — 4 BAR.** You may attempt a Disarm without dealing damage. On margin 8+, choose where the disarmed object lands within a plausible nearby space.

## Level 3 — High Integration

**Counter-Ambush — 6 BAR.** When an ambush or surprise attack begins and you are able to communicate, linked allies gain d4 as their ordinary Bonus Die on Initiative. Each linked ally may also ready one accessible weapon for 0 AP before the first normal turn begins.

**Precision Interdiction — 6 BAR.** When your Overwatch attack succeeds by margin 4+, choose normal damage or halt the target's current movement/advance if the fiction permits.

## Level 4 — Advanced II / Mastery

**Predictive Ballistics Matrix — 8 BAR.** Requires Mastery in one ranged Combat Skill. Against a target you have directly observed since the beginning of your previous turn, qualifying attacks with that Skill include its Skill Mastery Die.

**Close-Combat Forecast — 7 BAR.** Requires Mastery in Unarmed, Grappling, or an approved Melee Skill. Against a foe you have directly engaged or observed in close combat this round, qualifying rolls include the Skill Mastery Die.

## Level 5 — Elite

**Crossfire Geometry — 8 BAR.** Once per round when a linked ally attacks a target you can also threaten, spend your Reaction to either grant d6 as that ally's ordinary Bonus Die or immediately reposition a short distance before the attack resolves. Choose one.

**Kill-Chain Interrupt — 9 BAR.** Once per scene, when a visible enemy begins an attack or hostile maneuver, spend your Reaction and commit 1 AP from your next turn to make one eligible interrupting attack. If the attack succeeds, it may stop or alter the triggering maneuver when the fiction supports that outcome.

## Level 6 — Continuance

**Combat Synthesis — 11 BAR.** Requires one Mastered Combat Skill. Once per round after you legally use its Mastery Die, you may transfer the predictive solution to one linked ally. That ally gains d6 as the ordinary Bonus Die on one immediately related tactical roll. This never grants the ally Mastery.

# DEFENSE & MOBILITY

## Level 1 — Standard

**Balance Assist — 1 BAR.** Ordinary narrow footing, unstable surfaces, and controlled landings do not alone require a roll. Extreme or contested movement still does.

**Grip Assist — 1 BAR.** Maintain an ordinary human-manageable climb, hold, or tool grip with one hand when two would normally be required. Extreme loads still require the appropriate roll.

**Cover Protocol — 2 BAR.** After taking Cover, gain d4 as the ordinary Bonus Die on the first appropriate defense before your next turn.

**Combat Rescue — 3 BAR.** Carry, drag, or support an adjacent ally during your normal Move without automatically reducing your normal movement distance, provided the load is physically plausible.

## Level 2 — Advanced I

**Motion Prediction — 4 BAR.** Gain d4 as the ordinary Bonus Die on Dodge against a visible moving attacker whose motion you can track.

**Protective Intercept — 4 BAR.** Reaction: move a short distance to become an eligible target of an attack directed at an adjacent or immediately reachable ally.

**Adrenal Control — 4 BAR.** Once per scene, spend 1 AP to clear one temporary physiological/shock-based Stunned, Disoriented, or equivalent condition affecting you.

## Level 3 — High Integration

**Breach Window — 6 BAR.** After a team breach or forced entry succeeds, you may Move through the opening for 0 AP. Linked allies may spend their Reaction to follow a short distance if space and timing permit.

**Tactical Reposition — 6 BAR.** After a visible enemy misses you with an attack, spend your Reaction to Move a short distance.

## Level 4 — Advanced II / Mastery

**Kinetic Mastery Suite — 8 BAR.** Requires Mastery in Athletics, Acrobatics, or an approved movement Skill. During dangerous movement, climbing, jumping, pursuit, escape, swimming, or comparable physical traversal, qualifying rolls include the Skill Mastery Die.

**Evasion Forecast — 8 BAR.** Requires Mastery in Dodge, Reflex, or an approved defensive Skill. Against a threat you can perceive before resolution, qualifying defenses include the Skill Mastery Die.

## Level 5 — Elite

**Rescue Cascade — 8 BAR.** Once per round after you successfully protect, stabilize, move, or extract an ally, either you or that ally may immediately Move a short distance for 0 AP.

**Momentum Reserve — 9 BAR.** Once per scene, after you spend all 3 AP on movement, rescue, or protective actions, take one additional non-Attack Interact, Take Cover, or Guard action for 0 AP.

## Level 6 — Continuance

**Survival Envelope — 11 BAR.** Requires a Mastered Defense or movement Skill. Once per scene when a linked ally in short range would suffer a catastrophic physical consequence, spend your Reaction to attempt an appropriate Mastery-enabled interception, movement, or defense roll. Success changes or redirects the consequence; it does not erase the underlying hazard.

# RECON & INVESTIGATION

## Level 1 — Standard

**Sensory Filter — 1 BAR.** Select one distracting sensory channel for the scene. That distraction alone does not impose Disadvantage or force a roll.

**Low-Light Adaptation — 2 BAR.** After brief adjustment, dim light alone does not impose Disadvantage on vision-based Perception. True darkness and concealment still matter.

**Memory Cueing — 2 BAR.** Once per scene, recall one reasonably learned or personally observed detail without a roll.

**Pattern Index — 3 BAR.** Once per scene after obtaining at least three relevant clues, ask which two appear connected or which one most clearly fails to fit. The answer is bounded by evidence already obtained.

## Level 2 — Advanced I

**Counterintelligence — 4 BAR.** Once per scene after meaningful observation, ask one: Are we being watched? What evidence looks planted? What part of our cover appears compromised? The answer must come from detectable evidence.

**Route Modeling — 4 BAR.** After surveying known terrain or maps, ask for the fastest, safest, or least-observed plausible route among known options.

**Microexpression Read — 4 BAR.** After a short face-to-face exchange, identify the target's dominant visible emotional posture. This does not reveal thoughts or guarantee deception detection.

## Level 3 — High Integration

**Multi-Track Attention — 5 BAR.** Maintain awareness of two active tasks, targets, or threats without ordinary minor distraction penalties. Severe overload can still impose consequences.

**Evidence Lattice — 6 BAR.** Once per scene after the team possesses at least four meaningful clues, organize them into a provisional timeline or relationship map. The GM identifies one contradiction, missing link, or unresolved relationship exposed by the evidence itself.

## Level 4 — Advanced II / Mastery

**Forensic Synthesis Matrix — 8 BAR.** Requires Mastery in Investigation, Science, Perception, or another approved investigative Skill. When directly analyzing evidence in that Skill's domain, qualifying rolls include the Skill Mastery Die.

**Predictive Search Model — 7 BAR.** Requires Mastery in Perception, Survival, Tradecraft, Investigation, or another approved search Skill. After establishing a meaningful evidence base, qualifying searches for a person, object, route, or hidden activity include the Skill Mastery Die.

## Level 5 — Elite

**Distributed Reconstruction — 8 BAR.** Once per scene, combine observations from up to three linked allies. Each supplies one fact they personally observed. Ask one bounded question about the shared pattern and receive a truthful answer supported by those observations.

**Counterfactual Engine — 9 BAR.** Once per scene after establishing a likely sequence of events, ask what single observed fact would be hardest to explain if that sequence were false. The answer must arise from existing evidence.

## Level 6 — Continuance

**Continuity Lens — 11 BAR.** Requires a Mastered investigative Skill. Once per mission, after personally examining a genuine continuity anomaly or Carrier problem, make an appropriate Mastery-enabled roll. On success, identify one actionable relationship among cause, Carrier, Exposure, and likely downstream consequence. It never identifies the morally correct decision.

# SOCIAL & COVER

## Level 1 — Standard

**Period Native — 2 BAR.** Choose one prepared period/culture for the mission. Routine etiquette, dress, slang, and institutions are familiar unless pressure, rarity, or scrutiny makes the outcome uncertain.

**Vocal Control — 1 BAR.** Maintain learned cadence, pacing, accent features, and vocal control through ordinary stress. It does not teach a language or guarantee impersonation.

**Stress Governor — 2 BAR.** Ordinary fear, startle, or acute stress does not by itself prevent deliberate action or force a Will roll. Extreme psychological effects still function normally.

**Social Mirroring — 3 BAR.** After a short exchange, choose rapport, calm, or belonging. Ordinary mannerism mismatch does not impose Disadvantage on your first appropriate social approach to that target/group.

## Level 2 — Advanced I

**Deep Cover — 4 BAR.** A properly prepared cover identity passes routine verification without a roll. Unusual scrutiny, contradictory facts, or high-security checks can still require resolution.

**Behavioral Baseline — 4 BAR.** After observing a person in a normal interaction, later attempts to detect a meaningful departure from that baseline may use d4 as the ordinary Bonus Die when relevant.

**Credential Mesh — 5 BAR.** When the team has prepared a coherent cover package, routine cross-checks among its ordinary documents, roles, and stated relationships do not independently force additional rolls.

## Level 3 — High Integration

**Social Threading — 6 BAR.** Maintain two compatible social objectives, roles, or identities in the same scene without ordinary minor distraction penalties. Real contradictions still matter.

**Prepared Contingency — 6 BAR.** Once per mission, establish one plausible earlier social or logistical preparation consistent with your access, mission briefing, and events already established.

## Level 4 — Advanced II / Mastery

**Behavioral Mastery Model — 8 BAR.** Requires Mastery in Influence, Deception, Culture, Leadership, or an approved social Skill. After meaningful interaction establishes a behavioral model, qualifying rolls with that Skill include the Skill Mastery Die.

**Linguistic Immersion Matrix — 8 BAR.** Requires Mastery in Linguistics, Culture, or an approved language/identity Skill. After sufficient exposure or reference material, qualifying language, code, accent, and cultural-interpretation rolls include the Skill Mastery Die. It cannot create knowledge with no source.

## Level 5 — Elite

**Identity Continuity Engine — 9 BAR.** Once per scene when a prepared cover is challenged by a new but plausible question, establish one minor supporting detail consistent with everything already known. Major credentials, institutional access, or rare resources still require preparation.

**Consensus Shaping — 8 BAR.** When addressing a small group able to hear and understand you, one successful appropriate social roll may establish a shared immediate objective instead of persuading members one at a time. It cannot erase strong conflicting interests or compel impossible cooperation.

## Level 6 — Continuance

**Cultural Synthesis — 11 BAR.** Requires a Mastered Social/Cover Skill. Once per mission after substantial observation of a community, make a Mastery-enabled roll to identify one genuine value, taboo, obligation, or relationship that can open a plausible nonviolent path through the current problem. It does not dictate what the community chooses.

# TECHNICAL & MEDICAL

## Level 1 — Standard

**Jury-Rigger — 2 BAR.** Engineering may create a temporary period-plausible substitute from available materials when the task is physically feasible.

**Fine Motor Stabilizer — 1 BAR.** Ignore ordinary tremor, vibration, or stress instability during trained precision manual work.

**Rapid Triage — 3 BAR.** Stabilize an adjacent living target for 1 AP instead of 2 AP when using Medicine and appropriate gear.

**Chronometric Focus — 2 BAR.** Track several short intervals, sequences, or timed events with implant-grade precision. Interpretation still uses the relevant Skill.

## Level 2 — Advanced I

**Field Fabrication — 5 BAR.** A successful Engineering improvisation may produce a robust temporary device lasting an operation when materials, tools, and design permit.

**Diagnostic Overlay — 4 BAR.** After one Observe/diagnostic action, ask for the most immediate visible mechanical, electronic, structural, or physiological failure point in a system you can directly examine.

**Sterile Field — 4 BAR.** In improvised but controllable conditions, ordinary contamination alone does not impose Disadvantage on Medicine procedures.

## Level 3 — High Integration

**Systems Bridge — 6 BAR.** Interface two otherwise incompatible but understandable systems long enough to exchange one bounded signal, control, measurement, or data set if a physical/logical bridge is possible.

**Parallel Procedure — 6 BAR.** Maintain one ongoing technical/medical process while performing a second related task without ordinary minor distraction penalties.

## Level 4 — Advanced II / Mastery

**Technical Mastery Kernel — 8 BAR.** Requires Mastery in Engineering, Computing, Security, Science, or an approved technical Skill. During hands-on analysis, repair, bypass, fabrication, or experimentation in the mastered domain, qualifying rolls include the Skill Mastery Die.

**Clinical Mastery Kernel — 8 BAR.** Requires Mastery in Medicine, Recovery, or an approved clinical Skill. During diagnosis, stabilization, treatment, or controlled physiological intervention, qualifying rolls include the Skill Mastery Die.

## Level 5 — Elite

**Adaptive Fabrication Model — 9 BAR.** Once per scene after examining a failed or unavailable component, identify a plausible substitute architecture. A successful Engineering/Science task may build it if suitable materials and time exist. This does not fabricate impossible matter or advanced components from nothing.

**Trauma Orchestration — 8 BAR.** When treating multiple casualties, one successful Medicine action may stabilize one target and allow the next linked ally's immediate Medicine/Recovery action on another target to use d6 as its ordinary Bonus Die.

## Level 6 — Continuance

**Restoration Synthesis — 11 BAR.** Requires a Mastered Technical/Medical Skill. Once per mission, when facing a complex failing system, structure, device, or patient with multiple simultaneous failure points, make a Mastery-enabled roll to identify a sequence that preserves the most critical function. It does not remove resource, time, or ethical tradeoffs.

# COMMAND & TEMPORAL

## Level 1 — Standard

**Command Link — 4 BAR.** Spend 1 AP. One linked ally may use d6 as the ordinary Bonus Die on their next tactical roll before the start of your next turn. Once per round.

**Motion Economy — 1 BAR.** Once per turn, Interact with one worn or immediately accessible ordinary item for 0 AP.

**Breath Control — 1 BAR.** Extend ordinary breath-holding and remain functional through brief smoke/oxygen stress. Extreme exposure still uses Fortitude or the appropriate rule.

**Thanatic Anchor — 4 BAR.** With explicit mission authorization and continuous observation, maintain the precision surveillance/insertion lock required for Aletheia's terminal-host implant procedure. It does not select the host or guarantee physical access.

## Level 2 — Advanced I

**Pain Compartmentalization — 5 BAR.** Once per scene, ignore one temporary injury-related Disadvantage until the end of your next turn.

**Team Clock — 4 BAR.** Linked allies share precise timing cues. Once per scene, the team may coordinate one simultaneous mundane action without timing uncertainty alone forcing separate setup rolls.

**Mission Cache — 5 BAR.** Once per mission, identify one previously authorized ordinary resource, contact method, or cache Aletheia could plausibly have arranged before insertion. The GM defines its limits.

## Level 3 — High Integration

**Temporal Intuition — 7 BAR.** Once per mission, ask whether a discovered fact, location, or event has a meaningful relationship to the mission's known temporal fulcrum. The answer is directional, not prescriptive.

**Coordination Mesh — 6 BAR.** Once per round when a linked ally succeeds on a setup, Assist, Guard, or tactical coordination action, another linked ally may use d4 as the ordinary Bonus Die on one immediately related roll.

## Level 4 — Advanced II / Mastery

**Command Mastery Mesh — 8 BAR.** Requires Mastery in Leadership, Tactics, Initiative, or an approved command Skill. When coordinating multiple actors under immediate pressure, qualifying rolls include the Skill Mastery Die.

**Temporal Analysis Mastery — 9 BAR.** Requires Mastery in History, Science, Investigation, or another approved temporal-analysis Skill. When analyzing an actual continuity anomaly with sufficient evidence, qualifying rolls include the Skill Mastery Die. It cannot predict an unknowable future.

## Level 5 — Elite

**Aletheian Intuition — 10 BAR.** Once per mission, request one concise encoded directional insight from Aletheia. It identifies a useful relationship or risk but never supplies the correct moral or strategic choice.

**Distributed Command — 9 BAR.** Once per scene, spend 1 AP to designate a shared objective. Until your next turn, the first three linked allies who take materially different actions toward it may each use d4 as their ordinary Bonus Die if they do not already have a stronger ordinary Bonus Die.

## Level 6 — Continuance

**Continuance Mesh — 12 BAR.** Requires a Mastered Command/Temporal Skill. Once per mission during a multi-front continuity crisis, establish up to three simultaneous objectives. Until the crisis phase changes, linked allies can identify which objective their action supports. Once per round, one qualifying roll toward each distinct objective may use d4 as its ordinary Bonus Die. The Mesh organizes choices; it never chooses priorities for the team.

# Catalog rules and clarifications

## Bonus-die source

Unless a VAM explicitly grants access to a Skill Mastery Die, any die it adds is an **ordinary Bonus Die** and competes with Assist, ordinary gear, situational, and other Feature bonus dice under the v0.188 dice-source rule.

## Mastery

A Mastery-capable VAM does nothing special if the required Skill is not Mastered. It may still have no additional effect unless specifically stated. Mastery VAMs are therefore deliberate BAR commitments attached to a character's advancement choices.

## Authorization

A Vector may load only VAMs at or below their current authorization level. Loading a legal VAM into unused BAR remains free. Replacing already-loaded VAMs during play costs 1 AP for the whole field swap under the existing candidate rule.

## BAR costs

BAR cost measures active integration burden, not power level. A high-level VAM can be inexpensive; a low-level VAM can be relatively expensive when its function is broad or demanding.

## Retired/absorbed v0.187 concepts

- **Steady Hands** is absorbed into Fine Motor Stabilizer.
- **Tactical Advance** and **Combat Mobility** are replaced by the new movement progression.
- **Weapon Stabilizer** is replaced by normal Aim interactions and Predictive Ballistics progression.
- **Field Analysis** is replaced by Evidence Lattice/Forensic Synthesis; no general VAM directly improves Carrier merely because a Science roll succeeds.
- **Emergency Protocols** is retired from the general catalog after the HP redesign; future death/trauma rules may reintroduce a narrower concept.
- **Zero-Window Entry** is retired as a general VAM because its action-economy effect was too broad; its identity is distributed among Breach Window, Momentum Reserve, and Kill-Chain Interrupt.

## Final candidate verdict

The catalog is structurally complete for v0.188 synchronization. Remaining changes should come from concrete app/rulebook implementation or full Adventure Path playtest findings, not from filling progression holes.
