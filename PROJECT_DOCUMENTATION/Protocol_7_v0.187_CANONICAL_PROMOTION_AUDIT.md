## Verified preserved locks
The v0.187 live artifacts preserve the audited baseline for:
- Protocol Dice blank/scoring procedure and standard DC/margin framework.
- Starting Ability array and 20 starting Skill Ranks.
- Three associated Ability dice per Skill and untrained d4.
- Cumulative Skill breakpoints / Progress Ranks.
- HP = CON maximum × 3.
- 3 AP and 1-AP normal attacks.
- 1 AP / 30-foot movement and no universal Run action.
- One normal Reaction with banked AP first and at most one borrowed AP.
- One primary gear die per roll.
- Armor as a defense-pool die rather than simultaneous damage reduction.
- 16 BAR capacity and VAM load/swap behavior.
- Historical Carrier states and Carrier-versus-Exposure tradeoff.
- Four mission outcomes based on Carrier success and Exposure target.
