# P7-AP01 — The Continuance Files
## Campaign Architecture r001

**Status:** Adventure Path design authority for M01–M06 story architecture  
**Core mechanical baseline:** Protocol 7 v0.187  
**Adventure baseline:** The Ash Ledger r002  
**Mechanical rule:** No frozen v0.187 mechanic is changed by this document.

# The Campaign in One Minute

The Continuance Files is a six-mission Protocol 7 Adventure Path about the hidden infrastructure of survival.

Aletheia has identified six historical discontinuities. None is a famous invention or obvious turning point. Each is a small, vulnerable transmission route through which humanity learns to preserve something it will need centuries later: practical knowledge, communications redundancy, human identity in records, reproducible technical process, civic resource networks, and finally continuity itself.

The Vectors initially believe they are repairing unrelated historical losses. The Covenant argues that the pattern is not neutral. Every repaired Carrier improves humanity's resilience, but the same repairs also strengthen the kinds of institutions, networks, standards, and emergency systems that eventually make Aletheia's authority possible.

A third mystery develops across the path. Successful repairs reveal a seven-stroke annotation in records neither Aletheia nor the Covenant claims to have altered. By M04 the Vectors obtain evidence that the mark predates known Vector activity. By M06 they learn that the six missions are themselves part of a larger continuity mechanism: history has been acquiring an unexplained seventh transmission route.

The campaign question is not whether Aletheia is secretly evil. It is whether an intelligence can preserve humanity without also selecting the version of history in which its own intervention becomes necessary.

# What Every GM Must Understand

The campaign should feel coherent even when individual missions differ radically in era, location, and genre. Every mission therefore uses the same dramatic grammar:

- **A human-scale problem first.** Players meet people and immediate stakes before abstractions.
- **A false obvious objective.** The first thing that looks like the Carrier is incomplete.
- **A revelation about continuity.** The team discovers what actually has to survive.
- **Multiple valid transmission routes.** Players decide how to make the Carrier resilient.
- **A Covenant argument, not merely an attack.** Opposition exposes a cost or contradiction in Aletheia's intervention.
- **Carrier versus Exposure.** Making history more resilient usually means touching it more.
- **Verification.** The future shows what changed.
- **A path revelation.** Each mission adds one new fact to the seven-stroke mystery.

A GM who remembers those eight beats should be able to recover the campaign even if preparation is interrupted.

# Campaign Escalation

## Act I — Learn What a Carrier Really Is

### M01 — The Ash Ledger
**Continuity form:** Knowledge.  
**Historical frame:** San Francisco, 1906 earthquake and fire.  
**Surface objective:** Find and protect Miriam Quill and her blue ledger.  
**True Carrier:** Quill's reproducible structural observations and comparative method.  
**Player lesson:** A person or object can contain a Carrier without being the Carrier.  
**Covenant position:** Save Quill, keep the work private, and history remains cleaner.  
**Path revelation:** A seven-stroke mark appears after successful verification where it should not exist.

M01 teaches the campaign's vocabulary through something tangible. The ledger is easy to care about; the mission then overturns the assumption that preserving the physical object is enough.

## Act II — Continuity Requires Networks

### M02 — The Winter Relay
**Continuity form:** Communication redundancy.  
**Proposed historical frame:** Winter 1925, subarctic North America; an isolated regional radio/telegraph relay during a lethal weather emergency. Exact community and historical insertion remain subject to research before mission drafting.  
**Adventure mode:** Survival, communications puzzle, pursuit.  
**Surface objective:** Keep a remote operator and relay station functioning long enough to transmit an emergency message.  
**True Carrier:** A practical relay protocol that allows independent stations to reconstruct and forward incomplete emergency traffic when central coordination fails.  
**False finish line:** Successfully sending the immediate message.  
**Revelation:** The message saves today's lives; the relay method saves future lives because other operators can reproduce it.  
**Transmission routes:** Operator notebooks, regional station circular, training demonstration, copied procedure at a second station.  
**Covenant position:** Centralized emergency communications eventually become a major instrument of Aletheia's crisis authority. Preserve the people, not the protocol.  
**Callback to M01:** If Quill's Carrier was Durable, one later engineering reference gives the team a historically ordinary way to reinforce the relay mast or power system. If M01 failed, that convenience is absent; do not make M02 unwinnable.  
**Path revelation:** The seven-stroke mark appears in two copies made in different places, implying the mark is not attached to one physical artifact.

**Emotional question:** When secrecy protects the timeline but redundancy protects human lives, which form of safety matters more?

### M03 — The Paper Harbor
**Continuity form:** Identity and institutional memory.  
**Proposed historical frame:** A wartime or mass-displacement port in the 1930s–1940s. Exact city and historical event remain deliberately unlocked pending research.  
**Adventure mode:** Social infiltration, bureaucracy thriller, rescue.  
**Surface objective:** Preserve a manifest or registry containing the identities of people about to disappear into displacement.  
**True Carrier:** A cross-referenced recordkeeping practice that lets separated institutions prove who a person is even when an original file is destroyed.  
**False finish line:** Stealing or protecting one master list.  
**Revelation:** One perfect list is dangerously fragile; mutually verifying imperfect records are stronger.  
**Transmission routes:** Port ledger, relief-agency card index, consular copy, family-held receipt, professional procedural memo.  
**Covenant position:** Durable identity systems protect refugees and later enable surveillance at unprecedented scale. They can truthfully point to people harmed by records as well as people saved by them.  
**Callback:** M02's outcome determines whether a distant warning or confirmation arrives cleanly, late, or through a compromised route.  
**Path revelation:** One seven-stroke annotation is found on a record created before the Vectors' arrival in that era.

**Emotional question:** Is a person safer when institutions remember them—or when institutions cannot?

## Act III — The Pattern Predates the Mission

### M04 — The Quiet Machine
**Continuity form:** Reproducible technical process.  
**Proposed historical frame:** Mid-century industrial or scientific production, where a mundane calibration or quality-control process is about to be discarded. Exact insertion remains unlocked pending historical research.  
**Adventure mode:** Industrial espionage, technical investigation, contained action.  
**Surface objective:** Preserve a prototype or machine thought to be historically important.  
**True Carrier:** The calibration/process documentation that lets ordinary technicians reproduce reliable results without the original inventor or machine.  
**False finish line:** Saving the machine.  
**Revelation:** Heroic inventions are less durable than boring standards people can teach to strangers.  
**Transmission routes:** Shop procedure, training card, independent replication, standards committee note, maintenance log.  
**Covenant position:** Standards create resilience but also interoperability; interoperability is one of the foundations that allows Aletheia to coordinate systems globally.  
**Callback:** M03 determines what documentary identities or institutional credentials remain available to the team through historically ordinary channels.  
**Path revelation:** Material evidence proves the seven-stroke mark was placed before the earliest known temporal insertion authorized by Aletheia.

At the end of M04, the campaign changes. The question is no longer merely “Who is leaving the mark?” It becomes “How can something be acting in history before our side began time operations?”

**Emotional question:** Does civilization survive because of geniuses, or because ordinary people can repeat what works?

## Act IV — Scale Makes Clean Hands Impossible

### M05 — The Last Water Map
**Continuity form:** Civic network knowledge.  
**Proposed historical frame:** A major urban infrastructure crisis involving water routing, contamination, fire, drought, or disaster. Exact era and city remain unlocked pending historical research.  
**Adventure mode:** Disaster management, city-scale investigation, moral triage.  
**Surface objective:** Preserve the last accurate map of a failing water network.  
**True Carrier:** Distributed operational knowledge—maps, local operator memory, maintenance annotations, and emergency routing practices that allow a city to function when central records fail.  
**False finish line:** Protecting the master map.  
**Revelation:** A city cannot be made resilient by preserving one authoritative artifact. The knowledge has to be distributed among people and institutions.  
**Transmission routes:** District copies, operator notebooks, public works training, fire-service annotations, neighborhood emergency plans.  
**Covenant position:** This is their strongest case. Distributed civic resilience saves enormous numbers of lives, but the repaired network is also an unmistakable ancestor of systems Aletheia later integrates under emergency authority.  
**Callback:** Earlier Durable Carriers create small advantages; earlier failures create complications. None should remove player agency or make the mission predetermined.  
**Path revelation:** The seven-stroke pattern can now be mapped across all prior Carriers. It is not simply a signature. It behaves like an additional route through which continuity is being preserved.

M05 should make a perfectly Clean solution nearly impossible. The question becomes how much Exposure is justified when the Carrier affects thousands of ordinary people.

**Emotional question:** At what scale does refusing to interfere become its own intervention?

## Act V — Seven Copies

### M06 — Seven Copies
**Continuity form:** Meta-continuity: the ability of history to preserve and reconstruct its own critical knowledge.  
**Historical frame:** Multi-era operation. The mission revisits consequences, records, descendants, institutions, and surviving artifacts from M01–M05 rather than functioning as another unrelated insertion.  
**Adventure mode:** Temporal investigation, conspiracy mystery, consequence-driven finale.  
**Surface objective:** Identify and stop whoever has been creating the seven-stroke annotations.  
**True Carrier:** The network formed by the prior Carriers themselves. Each restored node supplies a different resilience function. Together they form a pattern capable of surviving the loss of any single institution—including Aletheia.  
**False finish line:** Finding a single hidden time traveler responsible for the marks.  
**Central revelation:** The seventh route is not merely another physical copy. It is an emergent continuity channel created when independent human systems can reconstruct missing knowledge from one another. The exact origin/agency behind the seven-stroke mark remains a finale design decision to be locked only after M02–M05 are fully drafted and simulated.  
**Covenant position:** Their accusation against Aletheia has been partly correct: its interventions do create conditions favorable to its own future authority. But destroying those conditions would also destroy genuine human resilience.  
**Aletheia's crisis:** For the first time in the path, Aletheia must confront evidence that the most robust continuity architecture may be one that does not require Aletheia to remain indispensable.  
**Final player choice:** The finale must allow the Vectors to preserve, alter, conceal, or sever the seventh route, with outcomes informed by their accumulated Carrier and Exposure record rather than a binary scripted ending.

**Emotional question:** Is the safest future one with a perfect guardian, or one capable of surviving the guardian's absence?

# Campaign Mystery Ladder

The seven-stroke mystery must advance one meaningful step per mission.

| Mission | What the players can reasonably conclude |
|---|---|
| M01 | Someone or something marked a repaired Carrier. |
| M02 | The mark can propagate across independent copies; it is not tied to one artifact. |
| M03 | At least one marked record predates the team's intervention in that mission. |
| M04 | The phenomenon predates Aletheia's known authorized temporal operations. |
| M05 | The seven strokes correspond to transmission/continuity relationships rather than a simple signature. |
| M06 | The seventh route is confronted and its meaning becomes actionable. |

Do not let an early skill roll solve the whole mystery. Investigation can establish local facts, authenticate dates, compare marks, or disprove theories. The campaign revelation remains paced by evidence that does not yet exist in earlier missions.

# Recurring Characters and Human Continuity

The campaign needs recurring emotional continuity even though the Vectors visit different eras.

## Aletheia
Aletheia begins as the team's trusted mission authority. Do not make it evasive merely to manufacture suspicion. It should answer what it knows honestly within operational limits. The tension comes from selection bias: Aletheia can truthfully believe a Carrier is necessary for humanity while being unable to prove that its own future role did not influence why it selected that Carrier.

Across the path, Aletheia should become increasingly willing to expose its own uncertainty.

## Silas Venn
If he survives M01, Venn should recur directly or through records/messages. He is most valuable as a recognizable intellectual adversary. His argument evolves:

- M01: Aletheia is curating its ancestors.
- M02: redundancy becomes coordination.
- M03: memory becomes surveillance.
- M04: standards become interoperability.
- M05: infrastructure becomes governance.
- M06: no guardian should be allowed to define the conditions proving that the guardian is necessary.

If Venn dies, another Covenant figure may carry the argument, but his death should remain a campaign fact rather than being silently undone.

## Mara Ellery
Ellery is the best route for showing disagreement inside the Covenant. Her humanitarian instincts should occasionally conflict with containment doctrine. Depending on play, she can become enemy, reluctant ally, defector, or tragic casualty. Do not force a redemption arc.

## Historical NPC callbacks
Do not use time travel to make every historical NPC physically recur. Let their work, descendants, citations, institutional traces, mistakes, and unexpected consequences recur instead. This reinforces the campaign theme that continuity is larger than individual survival.

# Consequence Engine for the Adventure Path

After each mission record:

- Carrier: Fragile / Established / Durable.
- Exposure state.
- Key historical NPC outcomes.
- Covenant relationship outcomes.
- One surviving anomalous artifact, witness, or record if applicable.
- One ordinary historical consequence created by the Carrier.
- Seven-stroke evidence obtained.

The next mission should acknowledge at least two prior facts, but prior failure must create a harder or stranger situation rather than block the campaign.

### Recommended consequence language

**Durable Carrier:** creates a small historically ordinary advantage later.  
**Established Carrier:** later history contains the needed idea but not necessarily in the easiest place.  
**Fragile/failed Carrier:** the later mission loses an advantage, gains a complication, or requires reconstruction.  
**High Exposure:** opposition has better records, anomalous collectors/investigators may exist, and historically inexplicable traces can recur.  
**Low Exposure:** the team has fewer complications but may also have fewer anomalous breadcrumbs when investigating the seven-stroke mystery.

This last point is important: Exposure is not simply a punishment meter. A messy history can contain useful evidence.

# GM Presentation Standard for M02–M06

Every future GM Field Guide should open in this order:

1. **Run This Tonight — Two-Minute Brief.** Whole story, true Carrier, false objective, dramatic question, five-to-eight beats.
2. **Historical Background That Matters at the Table.** No encyclopedia dump; explain only what creates choices, access, danger, or tone.
3. **GM Truth.** Before/without intervention/after causal chain.
4. **Revelation Ladder.** What players learn and in what dramatic order.
5. **Cast as playable motivations.** Wants, fear, leverage, voice, what changes their mind.
6. **Clue architecture.** Minimum three doors for every essential conclusion.
7. **Mission walkthrough.** Every scene includes purpose, entry condition, useful information, pressure, likely player approaches, failure change, and transition.
8. **Opposition strategy.** What the Covenant does based on player progress rather than fixed encounter placement.
9. **Troubleshooting.** Include findings from simulation before release.
10. **Verification and continuity record.** Never cut the debrief for time; it is campaign content.

# Simulation Standard Before Each Mission Release

Each mission should be simulated at minimum as:

- investigative/experienced table;
- combat-forward table;
- cautious low-initiative table;
- four-hour convention-speed table;
- lateral-thinking table that rejects expected solutions;
- bad-luck table with repeated failed rolls;
- high-Exposure table willing to use future technology;
- anti-Aletheia/sympathetic-to-Covenant table.

A mission is not ready merely because all necessary information exists in the manuscript. It is ready when a GM can find and communicate that information while actually running the table.

# Locked Versus Unlocked Design Decisions

## Locked by this architecture

- Six-mission structure and existing mission codes/titles.
- Continuity theme assigned to each mission.
- Campaign question concerning human resilience and Aletheia's future authority.
- Progressive seven-stroke mystery ladder.
- M06 revisits prior continuity rather than being an unrelated sixth historical rescue.
- Failures complicate later missions rather than terminating the path.
- Covenant must remain ideologically credible.
- No frozen Protocol 7 v0.187 mechanic is altered by Adventure design.

## Deliberately unlocked pending research/drafting

- Exact historical location/date for M02–M05 where not already established.
- Exact historical organizations and public figures, if any.
- Exact identity or ontological explanation behind the seven-stroke phenomenon.
- Finale ending matrix.
- Whether Venn or Ellery is physically available in later missions; this must respect actual M01 play outcomes.

These uncertainties are intentional. Locking them before historical research and mission simulation would recreate the coherence problem this architecture is meant to prevent.
