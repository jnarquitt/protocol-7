# P7-AP01-M05 — The Last Water Map
## Nine-Profile Playtest Simulation r001

**Rules baseline:** Protocol 7 v0.187  
**Intended party:** Level 5  
**Status:** first simulation pass

## 1. Investigative / experienced
**Break found:** players correctly distrust the map immediately and risk skipping its false-objective phase.  
**Adjustment:** make the map genuinely useful by giving three actionable routes before the first contradiction. The reveal is not “maps are bad”; it is “a static authoritative map cannot contain a changing distributed system.”

## 2. Combat-forward
**Break found:** players may look for a human saboteur behind every failed water route.  
**Adjustment:** make the earthquake and infrastructure failure visibly real before Covenant opposition appears. Any combat must have a civic objective: hold a route, stop deliberate destruction, protect a crowd, or escape collapse.

## 3. Cautious / low-initiative
**Break found:** city-scale choices can produce paralysis.  
**Adjustment:** Samira always frames 2–3 concrete choices with visible consequences, never an open-ended “what do you do with Petra?” prompt.

## 4. Four-hour convention-speed
**Break found:** four knowledge nodes plus exploration can overrun the slot.  
**Adjustment:** two nodes are sufficient for Established progress; additional nodes strengthen Carrier durability. Combine Queue + Map if behind and preserve Verification/debrief.

## 5. Lateral-thinking
**Break found:** players may create their own messenger/training network instead of making map copies.  
**Adjustment:** explicitly support this. The Carrier is reconstructive capacity, not paper. Reward plausible teaching, paired custodians, verification walks, mnemonic systems, or other period-compatible solutions.

## 6. Bad-luck
**Break found:** repeated traversal/repair failures can make city infrastructure feel inaccessible.  
**Adjustment:** failure changes triage rather than access to the adventure. Lose time, one route, equipment, trust, or an information node; never lose the only route to the Carrier.

## 7. High-Exposure
**Break found:** Level 5 Vectors can plausibly brute-force rescue/repair with future capabilities.  
**Adjustment:** allow it. The mission's tension becomes historical footprint and Covenant recovery interest. Do not negate veteran competence simply to preserve difficulty.

## 8. Covenant-sympathetic / anti-Aletheia
**Break found:** Covenant's infrastructure-legibility argument is strong enough that players may prefer local compartmentation.  
**Adjustment:** this is valid. Test whether their chosen structure still creates enough overlapping independent knowledge for Established. A more local solution may trade Carrier durability for lower Exposure and reduced centralized legibility.

## 9. Infrastructure-blind table
**Break found:** terms such as cistern, conduit, reservoir, bypass and distribution route can become abstract quickly.  
**Adjustment:** every scene states the consequence in ordinary language. Use a simple node-and-line sketch. Read-aloud repeatedly shows water physically: jars, outlets, dry basins, leaks, pipes, storage, queues. No player must understand hydraulic engineering to make an informed choice.

# Advancement / Level 5 observations

M05 should test veteran breadth rather than merely higher numerical difficulty. Record during human playtests:
- frequency of VAM reconfiguration and whether 16 BAR creates meaningful choices;
- whether improved Skill/Ability dice visibly change reliability;
- whether HP advancement matters during disaster action;
- whether three AP still generates meaningful tactical tradeoffs at Level 5;
- whether specialists and generalists both receive spotlight;
- whether veteran Vectors can solve one crisis decisively while still being unable to solve every crisis personally.

If Level 5 does not feel materially more capable than Level 1, do not compensate by raising M05 targets. Flag the advancement curve for the post-first-pass Style & Balance Audit.

# Result

**AMBER-GREEN / playtest-ready first pass.** The adventure now has a distinct physical/exploration identity, a comprehensible Carrier revelation, substantial unfamiliar-setting orientation, and an explicit veteran-level test. Human playtest remains necessary before final promotion.
