# Protocol 7 v0.188 — Combat Carry-Forward Authority r001

**Status:** ACTIVE PLAYTEST AUTHORITY
**Purpose:** Make the unchanged promoted combat baseline explicit so publication does not have to guess from v0.187.

This file carries forward only combat rules explicitly supported by the v0.187 Canonical Playtest Locks and not superseded by v0.188.

## Turn economy

A Vector normally has **3 AP per turn**.

A normal Attack costs **1 AP**. Multiple normal attacks are permitted by paying 1 AP for each unless a specific action, weapon, VAM, condition, or other rule says otherwise.

Movement costs **1 AP for up to 30 feet** and may be repeated. Therefore 1/2/3 AP can normally produce up to 30/60/90 feet of movement. There is no universal Run action and no universal movement multiplier.

## Reaction

A Vector has **one normal Reaction between turns**.

When a Reaction requires AP payment, spend **banked unspent AP from the previous/current turn first**. Only if no banked AP remains may the Vector borrow **at most 1 AP from the next turn**.

Borrowed AP reduces the AP available on that next turn.

A rule that gives a new way to spend a Reaction does not automatically grant an additional Reaction.

## Active defense

Protocol 7 uses active defense pools rather than a universal passive Armor Class.

The applicable Defense Skill and its three Ability dice form the core defense pool. Relevant Gear, VAMs, Advantage/Disadvantage, conditions, and legal Mastery access then modify the pool using their named-source rules.

## Armor

Armor normally contributes its **listed Gear die** to an appropriate defense pool.

Armor does **not** also reduce damage unless that armor or another explicit rule specifically says it does.

The ordinary Gear limit remains one primary relevant Gear die per roll unless an explicit rule permits stacking.

## Relationship to v0.188 changes

This carry-forward authority does not restore superseded v0.187 rules. In particular it does not restore Constitution-derived HP, older Skill progression beyond d12, generic modifiers, or any universal Run rule.

Damage values, weapon profiles, critical procedure, individual attack/defense mappings, and specialized combat actions still require extraction from the promoted player-facing combat source if they are not already present in a current structured registry. They must not be invented merely to make this document appear complete.
