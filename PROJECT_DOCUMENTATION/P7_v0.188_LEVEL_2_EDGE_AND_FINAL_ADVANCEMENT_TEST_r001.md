# Protocol 7 v0.188 — Level 2 Edge & Final Advancement Test r001

**Status:** SIMULATION CANDIDATE

## Change to candidate progression

Level 2 now tests a universal, non-binding identity choice using the Protocol Dice Engine working **Edge** mechanic.

An Edge is not a Discipline, class, archetype, Path, prerequisite, or VAM category. It is a small character-defining benefit chosen at Level 2 and may be freely renamed/reskinned by the player.

## Protocol 7 candidate Edges

### Driven
Once per session, reroll one die you rolled. Keep the new result.

### Inspired
Once per session, after seeing your initial dice, add d4 to one eligible roll. This die follows normal dice-source and practical-ceiling rules and cannot be used to create routine seven-plus-die pools.

### Prepared
Once per session, establish one small plausible preparation, ordinary resource, or readiness detail that the Vector could reasonably have arranged before the current problem. It cannot create rare technology, erase Exposure, or retroactively solve a major obstacle.

### Connected
Once per session, invoke a plausible useful contact, relationship, introduction, or communication channel appropriate to the mission and era. It creates access/opportunity, not automatic cooperation or success.

### Durable
Instead of gaining a once-per-session Edge resource, permanently gain **+5 maximum HP**.

The +5 HP is an increase to a resource maximum, not a flat bonus to a roll, defense, damage, or action. It therefore does not violate the Protocol Dice no-flat-modifier design rule.

## Preliminary balance read

The Edge is deliberately below Mastery in power and does not alter the Level 4 watershed.

Durable creates a meaningful trade:
- low-STR L2 candidate: 20 HP becomes 25;
- typical d6 STR L2 candidate: 21 becomes 26;
- d8 STR L2 candidate: 22 becomes 27.

The +5 is substantial early, but it never refreshes and becomes a smaller proportion of total HP as the campaign advances. A typical unchanged d6 Vector reaches 33 HP at Level 6 without Durable and 38 with it.

Reroll and d4 options remain valuable throughout the campaign because they can be held for a decisive roll. Prepared and Connected provide horizontal power that can bypass or reshape problems without changing core dice math.

## Assist resolution candidate

For final integrated testing, **Assist dice occupy the same ordinary bonus-die slot as Feature dice** unless a rule explicitly says otherwise.

A roll therefore normally contains:
- its Core Pool;
- at most one ordinary bonus die from Feature/Assist/gear/situation sources, choosing the applicable source;
- a Skill Mastery Die when legally accessed.

This protects the intended routine six-die ceiling. Effects that do not add dice may still combine normally when legal.

## Level 6 Mastery candidate

Test **a second Mastered Skill at Level 6**.

This does not mean two Mastery dice can enter one roll. A roll uses only the Mastery Die associated with the Skill actually being rolled. The second Mastered Skill broadens veteran identity rather than raising the pool ceiling.

This gives the six-Level arc a clear cadence:
- L1: establish the character;
- L2: declare an Edge/identity marker;
- L3: intrinsic Ability growth;
- L4: first Skill Mastery;
- L5: second intrinsic growth;
- L6: broaden Mastery and gain Continuance authorization.
