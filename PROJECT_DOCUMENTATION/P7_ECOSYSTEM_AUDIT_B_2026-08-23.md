### B-02 — PASS: M04 restores its assigned campaign function

The M04 r001 draft risked making the Antikythera mechanism itself the Carrier. M04 r002 explicitly corrects this. The machine is the false objective; the true Carrier is the reproducible evidence-and-description chain: observation, measurement, provenance, independent records, and enough preserved context for later investigators to challenge earlier interpretations.

This gives the promoted campaign a coherent progression:

- M01: a person/object can contain a Carrier without being the Carrier.
- M02: independent reproduction creates resilience.
- M03: resilience can require compartmentation and deliberate incompleteness.
- M04: evidence remains useful when independent strangers can reproduce and re-evaluate understanding.
