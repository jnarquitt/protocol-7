# Protocol 7 v0.188 — Ability Ceiling Decision r001

**Status:** GREEN CANDIDATE — synchronization required before public promotion
**Date:** 2026-08-23

## Decision

Protocol 7 v0.188 uses **d12 as the maximum Ability die**.

At Levels 3 and 5, Core Growth allows the player to increase one Ability die by one step:

**d4 → d6 → d8 → d10 → d12**

An Ability cannot exceed d12. If a player would increase an Ability that is already d12, the player must choose another Ability that can legally increase.

Core Growth does not alter prior HP gains and does not independently recalculate maximum HP. Under the current v0.188 advancement candidate, HP is governed by the Vitality Ritual at Level 1 and fixed +4 maximum HP at each later Level.

## Rationale

- The d12 ceiling is consistent with the Engine's existing die ladder.
- Ability growth occurs only twice in the six-Level Protocol 7 campaign, at Levels 3 and 5, so each increase remains a major intrinsic advancement event.
- A hard d12 ceiling prevents open-ended Core Pool inflation.
- It preserves Skill identity, Mastery, VAM authorization, BAR growth, and Edges as distinct advancement axes rather than allowing Ability escalation to dominate them.
- It closes the unresolved Ability-ceiling blocker in the v0.188 Advancement Candidate Specification without introducing a new progression axis.

## Synchronization consequence

The phrase **“subject to the final Engine Ability ceiling”** in the current v0.188 Advancement Candidate Specification is now resolved for Protocol 7 as **subject to a d12 Ability ceiling**.

This decision remains candidate authority until the synchronized v0.188 player-facing products are rebuilt, audited, and promoted together.
