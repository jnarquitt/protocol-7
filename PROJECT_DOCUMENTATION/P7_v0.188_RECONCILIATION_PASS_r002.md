## Work completed

### Skills
The 40-Skill registry was checked for count, category, Ability triples, d12 ceiling compatibility, and Mastery separation. No v0.188 design change required altering the inherited Ability triples in this pass. Registry promoted to reconciled playtest status.

### Conditions
The six-condition registry contained a stale model in which Suppressed and Disoriented carried their own d6/d4 Disadvantage dice. That conflicts with the v0.188 universal Disadvantage rule. Conditions now create named Disadvantage reasons; the universal operation removes the smallest Ability die after cancellation. No anonymous or alternate penalty die is created.

### Roll evaluator
The evaluator still said multi-source A/D interaction was unresolved. It is now synchronized to the rules source: reasons cancel one-for-one; remaining Advantage adds one d6; remaining Disadvantage removes the smallest Ability die; same-side reasons cap at one effective instance unless an explicit rule overrides.

### Reaction / armor predecessor recovery
The v0.187 canonical lock file was located and read. The following unchanged combat rules are safe predecessor carry-forwards unless v0.188 later explicitly supersedes them:
- one normal Reaction between turns;
- Reaction spends banked unspent AP first; if none remains it may borrow at most 1 AP from the next turn;
- one primary relevant Gear die per roll unless explicitly permitted otherwise;
- armor normally adds its listed Gear die to an appropriate defense pool and does not also reduce damage unless explicitly stated;
- normal Attack costs 1 AP and may be repeated by paying AP;
- 3 AP per turn.

### VAM audit — first deep pass
The 84-VAM catalog already follows the new named-source policy at its top-level schema and includes expensive Level 4 Mastery-access VAMs, which is directionally compatible with v0.188. However, it is not promoted yet.

Known reconciliation classes requiring full-catalog treatment:
1. Any VAM that grants/negates Advantage or Disadvantage must create or negate a named A/D reason, not define an alternate die size.
2. Any `ADD_DIE(source=VAM)` remains legal as a VAM die and must not be relabeled Advantage.
3. Mastery VAMs must require a Mastered Skill and their scope must be explicit.
4. Movement effects using vague `short` distance need either an explicit distance definition or a clearly rules-defined movement operation before publication.
5. Conditions referenced by VAMs must use exact registry IDs or an explicitly defined comparable effect.
6. AP-cost overrides and Reaction effects must be checked against the recovered action/Reaction baseline.
7. Narrative information VAMs must remain evidence-bounded and must not silently solve mission decisions.
