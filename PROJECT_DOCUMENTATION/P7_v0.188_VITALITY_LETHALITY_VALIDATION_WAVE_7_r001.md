# Protocol 7 v0.188 — Vitality Lethality Validation Wave 7 r001

**Status:** GREEN VALIDATION — HP architecture ready for candidate consolidation

## Scope

Wave 7 rechecks the encounter/lethality assumptions against the revised HP bands created by the Level 1 Vitality ritual and fixed +4 HP advancement.

Target bands:
- L1 ordinary: 22–26 HP
- L1 all-blanks exceptional: 28 HP
- L2 Durable ordinary: 31 HP maximum at L2 if starting at 26
- L6 ordinary: 42–46 HP
- L6 all-blanks exceptional: 48 HP
- L6 ordinary + Durable: 47–51 HP
- L6 all-blanks + Durable: 53 HP

## Design target

Protocol 7 should not balance danger by simply increasing weapon damage as characters gain Levels. Veteran danger should increasingly arise from better opposition pools, Mastery, coordinated actions, positioning, conditions, simultaneous objectives, mission clocks, Carrier/Exposure pressure, and consequences beyond personal HP.

## Level 1 result

The increase from the former 18-HP baseline to a 22–26 ordinary band materially improves tolerance for an unlucky early hit without making Level 1 Vectors feel invulnerable.

A four-HP spread between ordinary characters is small enough that experienced players should not perceive one unlucky Vitality ritual as a damaged build. The exceptional 28-HP result is only 2 HP above the ordinary maximum and therefore memorable without creating a separate durability tier.

**Finding: GREEN.** The new starting band is preferable to 18 fixed HP for campaign play.

## Level 6 result

Fixed +4 advancement preserves the original starting spread instead of compounding it. Ordinary L6 Vectors differ by at most 4 HP solely because of the Vitality ritual.

The 48-HP exceptional survivor remains within 2 HP of the ordinary maximum. The exceptional rule therefore remains flavorfully lucky rather than mathematically dominant.

Durable produces the expected dedicated toughness band. An ordinary Durable L6 Vector at 47–51 HP is approximately one meaningful hit tougher than a comparable non-Durable Vector, depending on weapon/effect profile. This is appropriate for surrendering a reusable Edge.

The theoretical 53-HP all-blanks + Durable Vector is deliberately the upper foreseeable baseline. It is durable but does not gain improved Defense, attack pools, AP, Reactions, BAR, Mastery, or action efficiency from those HP.

**Finding: GREEN.** No global damage increase is warranted.

## Encounter implications

### Ordinary historical opponents
Should remain dangerous through numbers, competent pools, cover, surprise, weapons, and concentrated action rather than inflated damage dice.

### Peer opposition
At Levels 4–6, selected elite opponents should gain Skill Mastery and veteran Features. This increases hit reliability and tactical pressure without requiring larger HP subtraction numbers.

### Boss / mission threats
Should pressure multiple resources and objectives. A 50-HP Vector should not trivialize a scene if the mission also contains civilians, clocks, escape routes, evidence, Exposure, environmental danger, or a target who can flee.

### Low-HP characters
A 22-HP L1 Vector remains viable. The game should not treat the lowest ordinary Vitality result as a flaw requiring compensation.

## Durable abuse check

Durable remains a Level 2 identity choice, not a prerequisite. Its +5 HP is strongest proportionally at low Level and becomes less dominant as universal +4 growth accumulates.

Do not allow repeated acquisition of Durable unless a future Engine rule explicitly creates repeatable Edges. Under the current candidate, an Edge is chosen once at Level 2.

## Advancement HP decision

Wave 7 validates the Wave 6 recommendation:

- Level 1 uses the six-Ability Vitality ritual.
- Starting HP = 18 + highest individual face.
- Ordinary range = 22–26.
- All six blanks = 28 HP.
- Levels 2–6 each grant +4 maximum HP.
- Durable grants +5 maximum HP once at Level 2 in place of a renewable Edge ability.

The former STR-derived advancement rule is superseded for the v0.188 candidate.

## Rulebook communication requirement

The rulebook should explicitly distinguish two jobs:

**Creation randomness discovers.** The Vitality ritual lets the dice reveal an intangible survival quality and creates a narrow, safe amount of individuality.

**Advancement rewards experience.** Fixed +4 HP prevents repeated random rolls from accumulating unfairness and represents the broad survival reserve gained through actual missions.

This is part of the Protocol Dice teaching philosophy, not merely a balance note.

## Verdict

**GREEN.** Replace the STR-derived HP section in the consolidated advancement candidate. No further HP redesign is required before the broader v0.188 integration pass unless later full-adventure simulations reveal a concrete lethality problem.
