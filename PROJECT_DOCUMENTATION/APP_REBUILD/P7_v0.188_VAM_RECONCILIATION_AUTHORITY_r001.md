# Protocol 7 v0.188 — VAM Reconciliation Authority r001

**Status:** ACTIVE OVERLAY AUTHORITY FOR THE 84-VAM CATALOG
**Applies to:** `P7_v0.188_VAM_CANONICAL_DATABASE_r001.json`
**Rules authority:** `../P7_v0.188_PLAYTEST_RULES_SOURCE_r001.md`

This pass reviewed all 84 VAM records across all six families. The underlying catalog remains useful and is retained. This overlay normalizes ambiguous operations without redesigning the catalog wholesale.

## Global interpretations

These interpretations apply to every VAM unless a VAM explicitly says otherwise.

### Distance
`short` and `short_range` in the candidate catalog mean **up to 30 feet**. This is a bounded VAM movement/range term; it does not create a universal movement category and does not change the normal rule of 30 feet per AP.

A VAM effect that says `MOVE distance: short` therefore permits up to 30 feet of movement at the timing and cost specified by that VAM. It does not multiply normal movement.

### Advantage and Disadvantage
`NEGATE_DISADVANTAGE_SOURCE` means remove one specifically identified Disadvantage reason before the normal A/D cancellation step. It does not add Advantage and does not add a die.

`GRANT_ADVANTAGE` means create one named Advantage reason. The universal v0.188 Advantage operation adds one d6 after cancellation/capping.

`IMPOSE_DISADVANTAGE` means create one named Disadvantage reason. The universal v0.188 Disadvantage operation removes the smallest Ability die after cancellation/capping.

`NEGATE_MINOR_DISTRACTION` and `NEGATE_HINDRANCE` remove only the named fictional hindrance described by the VAM. If that hindrance was the sole reason for Disadvantage, the Disadvantage reason disappears. These operations never create a positive die by themselves.

### VAM dice
Every `ADD_DIE` whose source is `VAM` is a true VAM die. It is not Advantage. Its listed die size is intentional and survives this reconciliation.

### Mastery
`ALLOW_MASTERY` never grants Mastery by itself. The rolled Skill must already be one of the Vector's Mastered Skills. The VAM merely provides legal access to the Mastery die for the stated scope.

A Mastery-enabled Level 6 effect likewise requires a Mastered Skill within the VAM's stated domain. Only one Mastery die may enter a roll.

### Reactions
A VAM requiring `reaction:1` consumes the Vector's one normal Reaction between turns. Pay for that Reaction using the normal v0.188 carry-forward rule: spend banked unspent AP first; only if none remains may the Vector borrow at most 1 AP from the next turn.

A VAM cannot create a second normal Reaction unless it explicitly says it grants an additional Reaction rather than merely providing a new way to spend the existing one. None of the 84 records in r001 grants an additional normal Reaction.

### Zero-AP actions
A VAM that sets an action's AP cost to 0 changes only that named action and only under its listed trigger/limit. It does not create an additional AP and does not make related actions free.

### Conditions
Catalog shorthand maps as follows:
- `SUPPRESSED` → `COND-SUPPRESSED`
- `GRAPPLE_OR_COMPARABLE_CLOSE_CONTROL` → normally `COND-GRAPPLED`; the GM may use a source-defined comparable close-control effect only when Grappled would clearly misdescribe the fiction.
- physiological/shock-based stunned → `COND-STUNNED`
- disoriented → `COND-DISORIENTED`

A VAM may remove a condition only when its scope explicitly covers that condition.

### Information effects
`REVEAL_INFORMATION`, `ASK_BOUNDED_QUESTION`, `IDENTIFY_ROUTE`, `IDENTIFY_CONTRADICTION`, and similar operations are evidence-bounded. They may organize, expose, or interpret information the Vector could legitimately derive from the stated evidence. They do not manufacture unknowable facts, dictate the correct moral/strategic choice, or replace a required mission decision.

### Established facts and resources
`ESTABLISH_FACT`, `ESTABLISH_RESOURCE`, and Prepared-style effects must be plausible, consistent with established events, and within the access described by the VAM. They cannot retroactively create unique mission-critical technology, erase a known obstacle, contradict a revealed fact, or independently resolve the mission.

## Playtest watch list

These are not blockers:
- Snap Shot may create a very efficient Aim/attack sequence and should be observed.
- Kill-Chain Interrupt has both Reaction payment and an explicit next-turn AP charge; observe whether its once-per-scene impact justifies that cost.
- Level 5–6 team-support VAMs can add several VAM dice across a round. This is intentional but should be watched for table-speed and pool inflation.
- 7–9 BAR Mastery VAMs consume a substantial fraction of Level 4 BAR 24; this is intentional investment pressure and should be tested rather than preemptively reduced.
