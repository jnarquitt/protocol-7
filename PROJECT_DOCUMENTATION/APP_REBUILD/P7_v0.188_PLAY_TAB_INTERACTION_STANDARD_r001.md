# Protocol 7 v0.188 — Play Tab Interaction Standard r001

**Status:** Active v0.188 app design standard; living playtest, not locked rules.

## Core interaction rule

**When a player does not know what to do, go to PLAY.**

PLAY is the sole action-resolution surface. The app must not create competing roll builders or separate rolling workflows elsewhere.

## What PLAY must surface

PLAY shows the Vector's current actionable state in one place:

- current Skills and their dice;
- loaded VAMs and actionable VAM effects;
- current Gear/loadout;
- current assembled dice pool;
- Difficulty and Advantage/Disadvantage controls;
- physical-dice pool display and digital Roll button;
- HP, AP, Reaction and current session-use resources;
- Vitality Spark / Perfect Equilibrium eligibility when relevant.

## Other tabs

Creation/management tabs exist to choose, purchase, load, unload, inspect, or advance character resources. They do **not** resolve actions and do **not** roll normal Skill pools.

Tapping a Skill, loaded VAM, or equipped Gear item outside PLAY may act as a shortcut. The shortcut must:

1. select or configure the relevant component;
2. navigate automatically to PLAY;
3. show the resulting pool there;
4. require the actual roll to happen from PLAY.

Players should not have to manually navigate to PLAY after tapping an actionable component.

## Consistency test

For every new app feature ask: **Does this create a second place to assemble or roll a pool?** If yes, redesign it as a shortcut into PLAY instead.

The mental model is intentionally simple: **Build the Vector elsewhere. Play the Vector on PLAY.**
