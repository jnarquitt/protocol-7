# Protocol 7 v0.188 — Level 1 VAM Presets Final Candidate r001

**Status:** GREEN CANDIDATE — player-facing synchronization pending

These fourteen presets are recommended starting packages under the v0.188 16-BAR Level 1 limit. They are not classes. A player may freely build any legal Standard VAM loadout.

The purpose of a preset is speed, identity, and a clear starting concept. Each also lists suggested later upgrades without creating a mandatory advancement path.

# Preset rules

- Presets use only Level 1 Standard VAMs.
- BAR totals are ceilings, not quotas.
- Players may swap any Standard VAMs during character creation as long as the final total is 16 BAR or less.
- Paths/presets never restrict Edge, Skill, Ability, Mastery, or later VAM choices.
- Suggested upgrades are examples only.
- A higher-Level character may continue using a starting preset as the foundation of a larger loadout; low-BAR Standard VAMs are intentionally useful throughout the campaign.

# Final candidate verdict

All fourteen starting identities remain distinct and legal under the rebuilt catalog. The catalog now provides clear L2–L4 advancement payoffs without stripping Level 1 characters of competence. This document supersedes the earlier simulation preset file where they conflict.
