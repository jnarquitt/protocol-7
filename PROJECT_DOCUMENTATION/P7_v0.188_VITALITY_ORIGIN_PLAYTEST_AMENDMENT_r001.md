# Protocol 7 v0.188 — Vitality Origin Playtest Amendment r001

**Status:** ACTIVE v0.188 PLAYTEST AMENDMENT
**Authority:** Supplements Section 9, Vitality and Hit Points, of the active v0.188 Playtest Rules Source. This is a deliberate new playtest rule, not recovery of an older mechanic.

## Vitality Ritual — Digital Convenience

The app may provide **ROLL ALL VITALITY DICE**. This rolls the Vector's six current Level-1 Ability dice together once for the Vitality Ritual: STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS, and CHA. It is mechanically identical to rolling those six physical dice together. Players may still use physical dice and enter/select the results.

## Vitality Origin — Once-Per-Session Benefit

If the Vitality Ritual establishes a Vitality Origin, that Origin now has a small mechanical identity benefit.

**Vitality Spark — once per session:** When making a Skill roll whose listed three Ability dice include the Vector's Vitality Origin Ability, after rolling the pool the player may reroll **one die showing a natural 1**. The new result must be kept. This does not add a die, does not create Advantage, and does not change the Skill's normal Ability mapping. Mark Vitality Spark used until the next session.

The rerolled die does not itself have to be the Ability die matching the Origin; eligibility comes from the Skill involving that Origin. This keeps the rule fast at the table and avoids requiring visually distinguishable duplicate Ability dice after the roll.

## Extraordinary All-Blanks Result

The all-six-blanks Vitality Ritual result remains **28 starting HP**. Because no single Ability produced the character's survival result, the Vector gains **Perfect Equilibrium** instead of choosing a Vitality Origin.

**Perfect Equilibrium — once per session:** After making any Skill roll, the player may reroll **one die showing a natural 1** and must keep the new result. Mark Perfect Equilibrium used until the next session.

Perfect Equilibrium is deliberately broader than Vitality Spark, but does not grant additional uses. The all-blanks result occurs only when all six starting Ability dice show 1–3. With the Level-1 array d8, d8, d6, d6, d4, d4, its probability is 81/4096, approximately **1.98%**. Its rarity plus the existing 28 HP result justifies the broader once-per-session trigger for this playtest.

## Math / Balance Note

A natural 1 contributes zero under Protocol Dice, so rerolling it has an expected gain equal to the ordinary expected Protocol contribution of the rerolled die: d4 = 1.00, d6 = 2.50, d8 = 3.75, d10 = 4.90, d12 = 5.83 points. The benefit is constrained by three gates: a natural 1 must actually be present, Vitality Spark requires the rolled Skill to include the Origin Ability, and either benefit is usable only once per session. Perfect Equilibrium removes only the Skill/Origin gate and remains once per session.

This rule should be specifically observed in role-play-heavy, combat-heavy, and investigation/skill-heavy sessions to determine whether players remember it, whether the Origin restriction creates meaningful identity, and whether Perfect Equilibrium feels special without dominating play.
