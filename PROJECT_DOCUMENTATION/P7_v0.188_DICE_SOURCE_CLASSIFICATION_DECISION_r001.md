# Protocol 7 v0.188 — Dice Source Classification Decision r001

**Status:** GREEN CANDIDATE — synchronization required before public promotion
**Date:** 2026-08-23

## Decision

Protocol 7 v0.188 does **not** use a generic ordinary Bonus Die slot.

Dice added to or removed from a roll come only from explicit, identifiable sources:

1. **Core Pool** — the Skill die plus the action's associated Ability dice under the Protocol Dice rules.
2. **Gear Die** — one primary relevant gear die when the gear materially contributes to the action.
3. **VAM Dice** — only when a specific VAM explicitly adds a die to the pool or explicitly grants access to a Mastery Die.
4. **Advantage / Disadvantage Dice** — dice added to or removed from the pool by the GM's situational judgment or by a specific rule that automatically grants Advantage or Disadvantage.
5. **Skill Mastery Die** — when the Skill is Mastered and an appropriate Mastery-access VAM explicitly enables it.

There is no free-floating catch-all bonus-die category. A rule that adds a die must state why that die exists.

## Gear

A relevant equipped item may contribute its listed gear die when it materially assists the action. Gear is its own source and is not 'Advantage.'

By default, only one primary relevant gear item contributes to a single roll unless a rule explicitly permits multiple gear dice.

## VAM-added dice

A VAM may add a die only when its individual rules text explicitly says so. VAM dice are not a generic entitlement and do not arise merely because a VAM is loaded.

The final VAM machine-data catalog must therefore identify every VAM that adds a die, the die size, qualifying action/Skill, trigger, frequency, and whether it grants ordinary VAM dice or Skill Mastery access.

## Advantage / Disadvantage

Advantage and Disadvantage are situational pool adjustments, separate from gear and VAM dice.

The GM determines Advantage or Disadvantage from the fiction when circumstances materially favor or hinder the action. Specific rules may also automatically grant Advantage or Disadvantage for clearly defined reasons.

They are not numerical modifiers and are not substitutes for gear or VAM effects.

The app should make the reason visible whenever Advantage or Disadvantage is applied.

## Mastery

Skill Mastery remains its own explicit veteran source. In v0.188, ordinary Skill progression ends at d12. When a Mastered Skill is legally activated through an appropriate Mastery-access VAM, the Mastery Die equals the current Skill die, up to d12.

Mastery is not Advantage and does not consume or replace a relevant gear die.

## Roll-builder implication

The roll builder must present source-labeled components so a player can read exactly what is physically represented in the handful of dice:

- Skill
- Ability
- Gear
- VAM
- Advantage / Disadvantage
- Mastery

No die should enter or leave a pool without one of those explicit reasons or another future rule that names its own source class.

## Supersession

This decision supersedes v0.188 candidate text referring to:
- an 'ordinary Bonus Die slot';
- Feature / Assist / gear / situation competing for one generic slot;
- Inspired or another effect using an undefined ordinary bonus-die category.

Affected Edge and VAM text must be rewritten to state its actual die-source behavior before promotion.
