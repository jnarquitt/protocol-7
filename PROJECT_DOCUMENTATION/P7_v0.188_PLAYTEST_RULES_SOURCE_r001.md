## Protocol Dice
Faces 1–3 are blanks. Faces 4+ contribute face value. A normal Skill pool is the Skill die plus the Skill's three listed Ability dice. Repeated Ability references are intentional. Additional dice require named sources: one primary relevant Gear die, an explicit VAM die, legal Mastery access, or Advantage. There is no generic bonus-die slot.

Difficulties: Easy under pressure 8; Moderate 12; Hard 16; Very Hard 20; Extreme 24. Winning margins: 1–3 Narrow; 4–7 Solid; 8–11 Powerful; 12+ Overwhelming.

## Abilities and Skills
Level 1 Ability array: d8, d8, d6, d6, d4, d4. Ability progression: d4 → d6 → d8 → d10 → d12. Levels 3 and 5 increase one Ability one step.

Starting characters receive 20 Skill Ranks; each later Level grants 4. Untrained d4; ranks 1–2 d6; 3–5 d8; 6–9 d10; 10+ d12.

**Archery — DEX / DEX / WIS.** Bows and crossbows across historical eras. Precision Weapons remains for specialized precision ranged systems such as sniper/marksman weapons.

**Riding — DEX / WIS / CON.** Riding, controlling and maneuvering ordinary trained mounts under pressure. Routine travel on an appropriate trained mount does not require a roll. Unusual animals, mounted stunts, panic, pursuit, combat maneuvering or hazardous terrain can require Riding. Mounted attacks still use the weapon Skill; Riding resolves control/positioning rather than replacing Archery or melee training.

Skill Mastery: choose one Mastered Skill at Level 4 and a second at Level 6. Mastery does not automatically add a die. A Mastery die requires the Skill to be Mastered and explicit Mastery access, normally through an appropriate VAM. When legal, it equals the Skill die. Only one Mastery die applies.

## Common action adjudication
These mappings are current playtest guidance intended to prevent unnecessary Skill proliferation.

- **Intimidate:** normally Influence. Deception may apply when the threat depends primarily on a convincing lie; another Skill only when the fictional method clearly makes it primary.
- **Read motives / sense intent:** normally Perception. Investigation may apply when interpreting accumulated evidence rather than immediate behavior.
- **Tail / shadow someone:** normally Stealth when remaining unseen is the challenge; Tradecraft when maintaining a plausible surveillance pattern, cover identity, handoffs or countersurveillance is the challenge.
- **Assist:** describe how the Vector helps. If the assistance is plausible and materially useful, the acting character gains Advantage on the relevant roll. One effective Assist is the ordinary limit; additional helpers do not stack more Advantage. If helping is itself risky or uncertain, the GM may require the helper to succeed at an appropriate check first.
- **Cover:** cover is fictional protection, not a generic bonus die. When cover can physically interfere with an incoming attack, it normally provides Advantage to the appropriate active defense. Armor remains a Gear die and is not replaced by cover. Cover that completely prevents a line/effect can make the attack impossible rather than merely granting Advantage.
- **Ready / reload:** routine readying, stowing, drawing or reloading that matters during action normally costs 1 AP unless Gear or a VAM explicitly says otherwise. Fast Draw and similar effects can override this. The GM should not charge AP for inconsequential handling outside action pressure.
- **Generic resistance:** use the Skill that represents the active response when one exists. Fortitude is the default for poison, disease, physical exhaustion and bodily endurance; Will is the default for coercive psychological pressure; Reflex is the default for sudden hazards requiring immediate bodily response. Environmental survival over time may instead use Survival when preparation and fieldcraft are central.

## Advantage / Disadvantage
Advantage adds one d6 Advantage die. Disadvantage removes the smallest Ability die in the pool; if tied, player chooses. Each caps at one effective instance and opposing reasons cancel one-for-one. Gear is not Advantage.

## Action Economy and Reaction
A Vector normally begins a turn with 3 AP. A normal Attack costs 1 AP and may be repeated. Movement is 30 feet per AP: 1 AP = 30 ft, 2 AP = 60 ft, 3 AP = 90 ft. There is no universal Run action or ×2 Run multiplier.

A Vector normally has one Reaction between turns. Reaction is not a fourth free AP. Unspent AP remains banked for Reaction until the next turn. Spend banked AP first; if none remains, at most 1 AP may be borrowed from the next turn. Borrowed AP reduces the next turn. Unspent banked AP expires when the next turn begins.

## Vitality and Hit Points — CURRENT EXPERIMENT
After assigning the six starting Ability dice, roll STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS and CHA once each. The app may roll all six simultaneously; this is identical to rolling six physical dice together. Faces 1–3 are blanks.

If at least one Ability shows 4+, starting HP = 18 + highest face. If several Abilities tie for highest qualifying face, choose one tied Ability as Vitality Origin. If all six dice are blank, starting HP = 28 and the Vector has Perfect Equilibrium.

**Vitality Spark — once/session:** after a Skill roll whose three listed Ability dice include the Vitality Origin Ability, if the pool contains a natural 1, reroll one die showing a natural 1 and keep the new result.

**Perfect Equilibrium — once/session:** after any Skill roll containing a natural 1, reroll one die showing a natural 1 and keep the new result.

All-six-blanks on the Level-1 array occurs 81/4096 ≈ 1.98%. A natural 1 contributes zero; expected improvement from rerolling it is d4 1.00, d6 2.50, d8 3.75, d10 4.90, d12 5.83. Each Level after 1 adds 4 maximum HP.

## Level Progression — CURRENT EXPERIMENT
Level 1: BAR 16; 20 Skill Ranks; Vitality Ritual.
Level 2: BAR 18; +4 HP; +4 Skill Ranks; choose one Edge.
Level 3: BAR 21; +4 HP; +4 Skill Ranks; increase one Ability one step.
Level 4: BAR 24; +4 HP; +4 Skill Ranks; Master one Skill.
Level 5: BAR 27; +4 HP; +4 Skill Ranks; increase one Ability one step.
Level 6: BAR 30; +4 HP; +4 Skill Ranks; Master a second Skill.

Level 2 Edges: Driven — once/session reroll one die after rolling and keep new result. Inspired — once/session gain Advantage before a roll. Prepared — once/session declare reasonable ordinary preparation. Connected — once/session establish a plausible useful contact/introduction/social access. Durable — persistent +5 maximum HP.

## BAR and VAMs
BAR is loaded capacity, not fuel. Loading into unused BAR costs 0 AP. During active play, replacing/unloading already-loaded VAMs as a field reconfiguration costs 1 AP for the whole swap. A VAM cannot exceed BAR or authorization. VAM dice remain named VAM dice; VAMs that negate Disadvantage remove a named reason rather than granting Advantage; Mastery VAMs provide access rather than mastering a Skill.

## Gear, Armor, Combat and Damage
One primary relevant Gear die is the ordinary limit unless a rule permits another source. Armor adds its listed Gear die to an appropriate active defense pool when it can physically protect against the threat; it normally does not then subtract damage again.

Combat uses opposed attack and active-defense pools. If attacker total does not exceed defense, no normal damage. If attacker wins, normal damage equals winning margin. v0.188 currently has no universal critical subsystem; Matched Blanks remain disabled.

## Conditions
Current registry: Prone, Grappled, Suppressed, Stunned, Disoriented, Restrained. Conditions use named rules language rather than hidden penalty dice. Stunned removes Reaction and increases AP cost of the first action next turn by 1.

## Character Creation
Choose Custom or preconfigured Vector; enter name; assign d8/d8/d6/d6/d4/d4; perform Vitality Ritual; spend 20 Skill Ranks; load legal starting VAMs up to 16 BAR; select Gear; review pools and derived values. Preconfigured Vectors are shortcuts, not exceptions.

## Historical Operations
Carrier states: Fragile → Established → Durable. Carrier strength and Exposure are competing objectives. Outcomes remain Clean Success, Exposed Success, Contained Failure, Exposed Failure. Mission procedure: Briefing → Insertion → Establish Cover → Investigate → Identify Leverage → Intervene → Establish Carrier → Contain Exposure → Extract → Historical Verification.

Tapping an actionable Skill, loaded VAM or equipped Gear elsewhere may configure it and automatically navigate to PLAY, but must not create another roller.
