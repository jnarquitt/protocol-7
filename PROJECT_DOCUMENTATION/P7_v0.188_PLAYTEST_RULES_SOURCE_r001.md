## Current v0.188 mechanical baseline

### Protocol Dice
Faces 1–3 are blanks. Faces 4+ contribute face value. A normal Skill pool is the Skill die plus the Skill's three listed Ability dice. Repeated Ability references are intentional. Additional dice require named sources: one primary relevant Gear die, an explicit VAM die, legal Mastery access, or Advantage. There is no generic bonus-die slot.

Difficulties: Easy under pressure 8; Moderate 12; Hard 16; Very Hard 20; Extreme 24. Winning margin descriptions: 1–3 Narrow; 4–7 Solid; 8–11 Powerful; 12+ Overwhelming.

### Abilities and Skills
Level 1 Ability array: d8, d8, d6, d6, d4, d4. Ability progression: d4 → d6 → d8 → d10 → d12. Levels 3 and 5 increase one Ability one step.

Starting characters receive 20 Skill Ranks; each later Level grants 4. Untrained d4; ranks 1–2 d6; 3–5 d8; 6–9 d10; 10+ d12. d12 is the ordinary Skill ceiling.

Skill Mastery: choose one Mastered Skill at Level 4 and a second at Level 6. Mastery does not automatically add a die. A Mastery die requires the Skill to be Mastered and explicit Mastery access, normally through an appropriate VAM. When legal, the Mastery die equals the Skill die. Only one Mastery die applies.

### Advantage / Disadvantage
Advantage adds one d6 Advantage die. Disadvantage removes the smallest Ability die in the pool; if tied, the player chooses. Each caps at one effective instance and opposing reasons cancel one-for-one. Gear is not Advantage.

### Action Economy and Reaction
A Vector normally begins a turn with 3 AP. A normal Attack costs 1 AP and may be repeated. Movement is 30 feet per AP: 1 AP = 30 ft, 2 AP = 60 ft, 3 AP = 90 ft. There is no universal Run action or ×2 Run multiplier.

A Vector normally has one Reaction between turns. Reaction is not a fourth free AP. Unspent AP remains banked for Reaction until the next turn. Spend banked AP first; if none remains, at most 1 AP may be borrowed from the next turn. Borrowed AP reduces the next turn. Unspent banked AP expires when the next turn begins.

### Vitality and Hit Points — CURRENT EXPERIMENT
After assigning the six starting Ability dice, roll STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS and CHA once each. The app may roll all six simultaneously with one button; this is identical to rolling the six physical dice together. Faces 1–3 are blanks.

If at least one Ability shows 4+, starting HP = 18 + highest face. If several Abilities tie for the highest qualifying face, choose one tied Ability as Vitality Origin. If all six dice are blank, starting HP = 28 and the Vector has Perfect Equilibrium rather than a normal Origin.

**Vitality Spark — once per session:** after making a Skill roll whose listed three Ability dice include the Vector's Vitality Origin Ability, if the pool contains a natural 1, reroll one die showing a natural 1 and keep the new result. Eligibility comes from the Skill involving the Origin; the rerolled die itself need not be the matching Ability die.

**Perfect Equilibrium — once per session:** after any Skill roll containing a natural 1, reroll one die showing a natural 1 and keep the new result.

These are active experiments, not locked rules. The all-six-blanks result on the Level-1 array occurs 81/4096 ≈ 1.98%. A natural 1 contributes zero, so the expected improvement from rerolling it is the ordinary expected Protocol contribution of that die: d4 1.00, d6 2.50, d8 3.75, d10 4.90, d12 5.83. Observe whether players remember the feature and whether the broader Perfect Equilibrium trigger feels proportionate to its rarity and 28 HP result.

Each Level after 1 adds 4 maximum HP.

### Level Progression — CURRENT EXPERIMENT
Level 1: BAR 16; 20 starting Skill Ranks; Vitality Ritual.
Level 2: BAR 18; +4 HP; +4 Skill Ranks; choose one Edge.
Level 3: BAR 21; +4 HP; +4 Skill Ranks; increase one Ability one step.
Level 4: BAR 24; +4 HP; +4 Skill Ranks; Master one Skill.
Level 5: BAR 27; +4 HP; +4 Skill Ranks; increase one Ability one step.
Level 6: BAR 30; +4 HP; +4 Skill Ranks; Master a second Skill.

Level 2 Edges: Driven — once/session reroll one die after rolling and keep new result. Inspired — once/session gain Advantage before a roll. Prepared — once/session declare reasonable ordinary preparation. Connected — once/session establish a plausible useful contact/introduction/social access. Durable — persistent +5 maximum HP. These remain playtest material.

### BAR and VAMs
BAR is loaded capacity, not fuel. Loading into unused BAR costs 0 AP. During active play, replacing/unloading already-loaded VAMs as a field reconfiguration costs 1 AP for the whole swap. A VAM cannot exceed BAR or authorization. The reconciled 84-VAM catalog currently uses six families: Combat; Defense & Mobility; Recon & Investigation; Social & Cover; Technical & Medical; Command & Temporal. VAM dice remain named VAM dice; VAMs that negate Disadvantage remove a named reason rather than granting Advantage; Mastery VAMs provide access rather than mastering a Skill.

### Gear, Armor, Combat and Damage
One primary relevant Gear die is the ordinary limit unless a rule permits another source. Armor adds its listed Gear die to an appropriate active defense pool when it can physically protect against the threat; it normally does not then subtract damage again.

Combat uses opposed attack and active-defense pools. If attacker total does not exceed defense, no normal damage. If attacker wins, normal damage equals winning margin. Explicit weapon/VAM/special effects operate as written. v0.188 currently has no universal critical subsystem; Matched Blanks remain disabled.

### Conditions
Current six-condition registry: Prone, Grappled, Suppressed, Stunned, Disoriented, Restrained. Conditions use named rules language rather than hidden penalty dice. Stunned removes Reaction and increases the AP cost of the first action next turn by 1. Other condition effects follow the current reconciled condition registry.

### Character Creation
Choose Custom or preconfigured Vector; enter name; assign d8/d8/d6/d6/d4/d4; perform Vitality Ritual; spend 20 Skill Ranks; load legal starting VAMs up to 16 BAR; select Gear; review pools and derived values. Preconfigured Vectors are shortcuts, not exceptions.

### Historical Operations
Carrier states: Fragile → Established → Durable. Carrier strength and Exposure are competing objectives. Outcomes remain Clean Success, Exposed Success, Contained Failure, Exposed Failure. Mission procedure: Briefing → Insertion → Establish Cover → Investigate → Identify Leverage → Intervene → Establish Carrier → Contain Exposure → Extract → Historical Verification.

## Living-document publication discipline

Nothing in v0.188 is considered final or locked unless the designer explicitly says it is. The purpose of publishing frequent current builds is rapid group feedback. During this larger pass:

- deliberate changes become part of the current v0.188 playtest state;
- obsolete mechanics must not silently reappear;
- probability-sensitive experiments receive math/simulation before use;
- the landing page should present one obvious **current v0.188 playtest release**, not a ladder of internal r00x builds;
- prior revisions may remain in repository history for rollback but do not need player-facing landing-page links;
- rulebook/interactive-guide synchronization may happen immediately when efficient or be accumulated into a dedicated synchronization pass, but known rule changes must be tracked and must not be forgotten;
- app behavior is implementation, not an independent rules authority;
- the designer decides when v0.188 is finalized and ready for any actual lock/promotion.
