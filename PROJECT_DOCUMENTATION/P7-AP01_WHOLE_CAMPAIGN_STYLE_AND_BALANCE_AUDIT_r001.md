# P7-AP01 — The Continuance Files
## Whole-Campaign Style & Balance Audit r001

**Date:** 2026-08-23
**Rules baseline:** Protocol 7 v0.187 PRE-BETA
**Campaign span:** M01–M06, Levels 1–6
**Status:** First-pass analytical audit; revision recommendations are not yet release locks.

## EXECUTIVE RESULT

**AMBER.** The campaign has strong historical and thematic variety, but its first four missions lean too consistently toward investigation → revelation → ethical choice. M05 and M06 successfully broaden the experience with exploration, rescue, environmental action, triage, and simultaneous objectives, but the path will benefit from a second-pass rebalance that adds more kinetic and spatial play to M02–M04 without turning Protocol 7 into a combat campaign.

The campaign's strongest identity is not combat versus investigation. It is **competent people solving consequential historical problems under incomplete information while deciding how much intervention is justified**. Rebalancing should protect that identity.

## FIRST-PASS BALANCE FINDINGS

### 1. ACTION / COMBAT

The path does not need mandatory combat in every mission. That would work against the setting and the Covenant's moral complexity.

However, M03 and M04 risk allowing long stretches where physically oriented Vectors have fewer meaningful decisions. Second-pass revisions should add objective-driven action rather than arbitrary enemies:

- moving a person or record through a changing Marseille route while authorities close access;
- protecting independent evidence during a fire, collapse, theft, port accident, or contested transfer;
- chase/interception scenes where success changes the Carrier state rather than simply removing HP;
- environmental threats that reward Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Defense skills, movement, AP, gear, and teamwork.

Target: at least one substantial kinetic set piece in every mission, with combat remaining optional where appropriate.

### 2. INVESTIGATION

Investigation is the most repeated campaign mode. This is appropriate to Protocol 7, but the structure can become predictable if every mission is:

1. Aletheia gives incomplete objective;
2. team investigates object/person;
3. team learns object/person is not true Carrier;
4. Covenant argues against propagation;
5. team chooses.

Second-pass revision must vary **when** the Carrier truth is understood. In some missions players should know the Carrier relatively early and spend the adventure figuring out how to preserve it. In others the false objective remains useful. M05 already helps by making reconstruction/triage the dominant challenge after the revelation.

### 3. EXPLORATION

Exploration is underrepresented in M02–M04 and becomes excellent in M05/M06. The campaign should seed more physical discovery earlier so the shift does not feel abrupt.

Recommended additions:

- M02: exterior storm route, mast/feed-line access, neighboring relay route or emergency courier choice;
- M03: Marseille district/port movement with changing safe routes and document chokepoints;
- M04: wreck/recovery environment, museum/workroom evidence geography, or physically separated evidence chain.

### 4. SOCIAL / ETHICAL PLAY

This is a campaign strength. The Covenant improves substantially when it helps immediate human survival while opposing long-term propagation. Preserve this.

Risk: repeated philosophical debate can become static. Whenever possible, attach ethical positions to concrete actions and costs. Venn should not merely make speeches; his preferred solution should visibly save something and endanger something else.

### 5. SURVIVAL / DISASTER

M01, M02, M05, and M06 use environmental pressure effectively. M03/M04 provide a useful tonal rest, so they do not need equivalent disaster intensity. Keep the alternation.

### 6. TECHNICAL PROBLEM SOLVING

Strong across the path. The GM must never require players themselves to possess radio, archival, archaeological, engineering, or ancient-water-system expertise. Character competence resolves technical execution; players make consequential decisions about method, risk, redundancy, evidence, routing, trust, and Exposure.

### 7. HISTORICAL VARIETY

Very strong:

- 1906 San Francisco;
- 1925 Alaska;
- 1940 Marseille;
- 1900–1902 Antikythera/Athens;
- 363 CE Petra;
- c. 9600 BCE Upper Mesopotamia.

Potential issue: chronology jumps backward sharply after M03. This is not inherently bad, but campaign framing should make clear that Aletheia is following Continuance relationships, not moving progressively through calendar history.

### 8. READ-ALOUD / ORIENTATION

M05 and M06 establish the desired high standard for unfamiliar settings. M01–M04 should receive a lighter version of the same treatment during coherence revision. The goal is not longer prose everywhere; it is enough sensory and social orientation that a GM does not need outside research to make the place feel real.

### 9. GM WORKLOAD

Later guides are easier to internalize because they front-load:

- the whole story;
- true Carrier;
- false objective;
- NPC wants;
- revelation ladder;
- clue redundancy;
- fail-forward handling;
- troubleshooting.

All six should ultimately share this information architecture even if their scenes differ.

### 10. LEVEL / POWER CURVE

The mission ladder is now Level 1–6. M05/M06 correctly begin treating higher-level difficulty as simultaneous solvable problems rather than inflated target numbers.

The dedicated Advancement Audit must determine whether actual advancement awards create enough difference between levels. This audit does not certify the mechanics yet.

## CAMPAIGN RHYTHM RECOMMENDATION

Desired emotional/physical rhythm after revision:

- **M01:** disaster + investigation + first moral complication;
- **M02:** survival/technical action + false victory + ethical negotiation;
- **M03:** tense social/investigative thriller + route/chase pressure;
- **M04:** exploration/evidence mystery + objective-driven physical set piece;
- **M05:** large-scale disaster exploration and civic triage;
- **M06:** full synthesis, high agency, rescue/action, philosophical finale.

This produces escalation without requiring each adventure to be louder than the last.

## COMBAT TARGET

Protocol 7 should not promise a fight every session. Across the six-mission path, combat should be:

- possible often enough that combat/defense builds matter;
- objective-driven rather than attritional;
- one tool among pursuit, rescue, sabotage prevention, environmental action, stealth, negotiation, and technical work;
- increasingly consequential at higher levels without becoming the campaign's default solution.

During second-pass simulation, specifically test a combat-specialist Vector for meaningful spotlight even in sessions where no attack roll occurs.

## SPOTLIGHT TARGET

Every mission should contain meaningful opportunities for:

- physical/action specialist;
- investigator/analyst;
- technical/fabrication specialist;
- social/interpreter specialist;
- stealth/security specialist;
- flexible VAM/BAR planning.

Not every archetype needs equal minutes in every scene. Across a mission, no character concept should feel like it brought the wrong sheet.

## REVISION PRIORITIES

### HIGH

1. Vary the timing/shape of Carrier revelations so the campaign formula is less visible.
2. Add objective-driven kinetic/spatial play to M03 and M04.
3. Add more exploration/physical routing to M02.
4. Bring M01–M04 GM quick-review/orientation toward the M05/M06 standard.
5. Complete the Level 1–6 Advancement & Power-Curve Audit before tuning encounter difficulty.

### MEDIUM

6. Ensure Covenant interventions are actions with benefits/costs, not repeated speeches.
7. Strengthen cross-mission callbacks that affect play rather than merely text.
8. Audit campaign length and session count; M06 being two sessions should feel like earned finale expansion.

### LOW / POLISH

9. Harmonize terminology and visual information architecture.
10. Add campaign-level GM pacing advice for breaks between missions and advancement moments.

## PASS / FAIL CRITERIA FOR SECOND PASS

The path is ready for release-candidate consideration only when simulation can show:

- no three consecutive missions feel primarily like the same investigation structure;
- physical/action-focused Vectors receive meaningful spotlight throughout the path;
- noncombat specialists remain equally important in action-heavy M05/M06;
- every mission contains exploration or spatial decision-making of some kind;
- every essential clue has redundant access;
- historical unfamiliarity never requires the GM to research outside the product to run the session;
- Level 1 and Level 6 play feel substantially different without target-number inflation;
- the final philosophical choice feels earned by prior play rather than introduced in M06.

## DISPOSITION

**AMBER — revision required, architecture sound.** No mission needs replacement. The next audit should be the dedicated **Level 1–6 Advancement & Power-Curve Audit**, after which the campaign can begin targeted second-pass revisions with mechanical expectations in hand.
