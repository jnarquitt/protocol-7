# P7-AP01-M04 — The Quiet Machine
## Playtest Simulation Report r002

**Rules baseline:** Protocol 7 v0.187  
**Frozen mechanics changed:** none  
**Historical frame tested:** Antikythera and Athens, 1900–1902

## Coherence finding
The r001 historical frame was strong, but its Carrier was too close to “save/understand the Antikythera mechanism,” while the campaign architecture assigns M04 the lesson of reproducible technical process. r002 keeps the historical frame and makes the actual Carrier the evidence-and-description chain: observation, measurements, provenance, independent copies, and competing interpretations.

This also protects historical agency. The Vectors do not discover the wreck, invent the mechanism, or give historical scholars the correct modern interpretation.

## Eight-profile simulation

- **Investigative/experienced:** Players tend to solve the mechanism. GM guidance now redirects from correct interpretation to preserving verifiable observations.
- **Combat-forward:** r001 offered little satisfying action except forcing Covenant violence. r002 makes recovery hazards and human rescue the physical stakes; Covenant combat occurs only through escalation.
- **Cautious/low initiative:** Pressure clocks now remove routes rather than stall the plot. Essential evidence has three doors.
- **Four-hour convention:** Scenes 1, 3, 4, 5, 6 form a complete compressed run; sorting can fold into Athens analysis.
- **Lateral-thinking:** Any historically plausible independent record counts by function. Players need not create the manuscript's exact sketch/measurement/provenance combination.
- **Bad luck:** Failure changes time, trust, route quality, damage, or Exposure. It never erases the only campaign clue.
- **High Exposure:** Future scanning can trivialize local analysis but leaves strong anomaly evidence for M05/M06.
- **Covenant-sympathetic:** Players can deliberately suppress one route and still reach Established with two independent routes. The game records the ideological choice rather than punishing it as incorrect play.

## Promotion verdict
**PLAYTEST-READY / AMBER-GREEN.** The adventure is coherent and runnable. Remaining risk is historical texture: named fictional NPCs should remain clearly fictional/composite, and historical exposition should stay subordinate to player choices.
