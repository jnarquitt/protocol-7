The Winter Relay

Player Briefing · r001
Mission Context

A public-health emergency is unfolding in Alaska. Real people are already carrying the burden of the historical response. You are not being sent to create their success.

Aletheia has identified a smaller discontinuity in the communications network surrounding the emergency. A remote relay station becomes unreliable at exactly the wrong time, and something useful that should survive the crisis disappears from history.

Apocryphon
The message arrives seven times and is complete only once. The woman who hears the missing words writes numbers in the margin. One station repeats her. Two stations remember her. When the storm closes the trail, the third station answers a question nobody sent.
Known Constraints
  • Do not manufacture or replace the achievements of the historical serum relay.
  • Period-safe competence is preferable to future technology.
  • Weather is expected to degrade transport and communication.
  • A successful immediate transmission may not by itself resolve the discontinuity.
Before Insertion

What can your Vector plausibly do at a remote station—repair, medicine, logistics, signals, security, administration, or survival? What future capability would your Vector expose if a message could save lives tonight?
