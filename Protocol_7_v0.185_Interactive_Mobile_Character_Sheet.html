How this works

You have exactly six dice to assign: two d8s, two d6s, and two d4s. Each Ability gets exactly one die. Tap one of the three die buttons under an Ability. The remaining-dice tray updates immediately.

Assign Ability Dice

You have exactly six Ability dice: two d8s, two d6s, and two d4s. Give exactly one die to each Ability. If you chose a full preconfiguration on Start Here, these dice are already assigned for that path and you may change them if you want.

CON-based HP
Maximum HP = CON die maximum × 3: d4 12 • d6 18 • d8 24 • d10 30 • d12 36.
HP and Constitution

Constitution sets maximum HP. If CON changes during play, maximum HP changes while damage already taken is preserved. There is no separate Strength-based HP advancement rule.

VAMs

Biocognitive Augmentation Rating (BAR)

Biocognitive Augmentation Rating (BAR) is the standard measure of the processing, integration, and neural-interface capacity required to operate a VAM. Each VAM has a BAR value representing how much of a Vector's available augmentation capacity it occupies while loaded. A standard Vector implant supports up to 16 BAR of active VAMs.

BAR measures capacity, not energy. A 4-BAR VAM occupies four points of the implant's available augmentation architecture while loaded; it does not consume four units of power. Unloading the VAM makes that capacity available again.

This VAM-tab selector changes only the loaded VAMs. Full preconfigured Abilities, Skills, and Path are chosen from Start Here.

0 / 16 BAR
Biocognitive Augmentation Rating

Loading a VAM into unused BAR capacity is free. During play, replacing one or more already-loaded VAMs is a 1 AP field swap. The whole swap costs 1 AP, not 1 AP per VAM.

Era-Neutral Equipment Grammar

How to Use This App at the Table

The app has character-building/reference tabs and two tabs intended for frequent use during a scene: Play and Roller. You do not need to return to character creation to make ordinary rolls.

Start: Begin or clear a character, choose a complete starting configuration or build from scratch, and move through character creation.
Vector: Review identity, Path/configuration, Ability dice, HP, and other core character information.
Skills: Buy and review Skills. Every Skill can be rolled here, including untrained Skills at d4. Pin useful untrained Skills so they remain available in Roller during play.
VAM: Build or review the Vector's loaded augmentation package, BAR use, and VAM options.
Gear: Select and equip era-appropriate weapons, armor, and tools. Equipped gear contributes its die automatically when it applies to a complete pool.
Play: This is the character-state and decision screen during scenes. Use it for Action Points (AP), Reactions, loaded VAM options, equipped gear, Ability Die Step Effects, and quick reference information. Current AP is shown directly in Turn Controls; Spend 1 AP and Restore 1 AP change it immediately. Play is deliberately not the main rolling screen.
Roller: This is the main rolling screen. Quick Rolls contains Initiative, every trained Skill, and pinned untrained Skills, grouped Initiative / Defense / Combat / General. Each one-tap pool displays its result immediately beside its own Roll button. Custom Pool is for unusual pools assembled manually.
Learn / Rules: Use this tab to teach the game, refresh procedures, and find concise table references. Use the guidebook when you need the expanded treatment.

You should be able to play Protocol 7 from this app. Use Play for character state and decisions, Roller for routine dice pools, and Learn / Rules when you need to teach or check a procedure. Physical Protocol Dice are strongly encouraged because the app keeps the table clear: your phone handles the character sheet and pool reference, while the space in front of you can be used almost entirely for rolling dice.

Using ordinary dice: treat 1, 2, and 3 as blank. Every result of 4 or higher scores at face value.

Armor: One Mechanical Job

Armor normally contributes its listed gear die to an appropriate defense pool when it can physically protect you. It does not also subtract damage after the roll. Separate damage reduction exists only when a specific item or rule says so.

Skill Ranks and Breakpoints

Skill Ranks are cumulative. The Skill die improves at 0 d4, 1 d6, 3 d8, 6 d10, 10 d12, 15 d12+d4, 21 d12+d6, 28 d12+d8, 36 d12+d10, 45 d12+d12.

Ranks between breakpoints are Progress Ranks. They count toward the next improvement but do not change the current Skill die until the breakpoint is reached. The Skills tab shows how many ranks remain.

Hit Points

Maximum HP = CON die maximum x 3.

CON d4 = 12 HP; CON d6 = 18 HP; CON d8 = 24 HP; CON d10 = 30 HP; CON d12 = 36 HP.

Changing CON changes maximum HP. During play, damage already taken is preserved.

Complete Pool: Current Procedure

1. Define the scope. State exactly what this roll is resolving and how the Vector is doing it.
2. Choose the Skill. The Skill determines the three associated Ability dice.
3. Add the three Ability dice. Apply current Die Step Effects first; repeated Abilities are added more than once.
4. Add the Skill die. Untrained Skills use d4.
5. Choose one primary relevant gear item. Add that item’s gear die. Compatible gear does not stack unless a rule explicitly permits it.
6. Apply situational dice. Add Help, Advantage, VAM, cover, or circumstance dice and apply Disadvantage normally.
7. Roll once. Results 1-3 are blank; add every 4+ at face value.
