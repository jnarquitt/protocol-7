# Protocol 7 — Locked Decisions

## 2026-09-04 — Jade Lion House Style
Flagship branding, covers, title pages, presentation art, and major product identity use a premium colored-pencil illustration language on warm paper with visible authored texture, disciplined composition, restrained ornament, and professional typography. Simplified utility marks must derive from the same identity.

## 2026-09-04 — Adventure Format
Continuance Files adventures should adopt the ZPA v2.1 operating principles where compatible: six-item Quick Start, explicit act Objective/Pressure/Exit Condition/DM Emphasis, fail-forward information design, usable GM cheat sheet, and clear outcome states.

## 2026-09-04 — Workflow
Josh grants standing permission for the active development operator to update repositories as needed during content/design work. The repository workflow is agent-agnostic: no named AI tool, vendor, model, or implementation resource is permanently required. Specialized implementation resources may be introduced later when Josh chooses or when a bounded capability gap justifies them.

## Preservation
Existing published revisions remain preserved. A visual/structural refresh does not silently authorize mechanics changes.
