P7-AP01 · The Continuance Files

The Continuance Files

Complete first-draft Campaign Director Guide

Campaign Promise

The Continuance Files is a six-mission Protocol 7 Adventure Path about the infrastructure beneath heroism. Vectors enter disasters and turning points where one anonymous connection determines whether useful knowledge survives. After every extraction, Historical Verification reveals that the past the players remember always contained their intervention.

The Complete Sanctuary Answer

The Sanctuaries have no single builder or construction date. Ancient communities selected refuge sites and developed survival grammars during abrupt climate stress near the Younger Dryas’ end. Later cultures independently extended water, records, mechanical, relay, structural, and governance systems. Aletheia’s era excavated, connected, enlarged, and industrialized those accumulated sites.

Six routes carry knowledge and material forward: structure, communication, human records, technical process, civic utilities, and plural governance. The seventh is temporal: future survivors transmit limited corrections backward through Apocrypha. No generation receives a complete blueprint, preventing any empire, faction, or machine from owning the whole Continuance.

The remaining mystery is honest rather than withheld: Aletheia did not originate the first correction. The oldest copy treats the current Vector team as already known.

Mission Sequence

M01 — The Ash Ledger

1906 San Francisco. Structural comparison survives through Miriam Quill and redundant copies. The team first sees its own headings in history.

M02 — The Winter Relay

1925 Alaska. Communication survives because authority and local judgment are distributed.

M03 — The Paper Harbor

1940 Marseille. Compartmented records save people without creating one capturable master list.

M04 — The Quiet Machine

1901 Antikythera recovery. Technical relationships, not an intact object, survive. The mark predates Aletheia.

M05 — The Last Water Map

Petra, 363 CE. Water survival is a community agreement embodied in distributed physical routes.

M06 — Seven Copies

c. 9600 BCE and the Sanctuary era. The team discovers the layered builders and chooses custody of the seventh route.

Aha Architecture

Quill’s headings recur in radio protocol and water map. The blank seventh position recurs as station, card, gear plate, game role, channel, and temporal route. The crowd photograph becomes silhouettes in an older copy. “Not missing. Waiting.” changes from oddity, to threat, to routing instruction. “Everyone who did not know what they were building” becomes the Continuance’s ethical design.

Tone and Theory Collage

Lost continents, ancient astronauts, catastrophic resets, secret orders, comet stories, sacred geometry, pole shifts, psychic inheritance, simulation arguments, and cosmic-cycle theories appear as vigorous in-world interpretations. Each enjoys one apparent victory and one decisive failure. The verified wonder does not make ancient people smaller: ordinary cultures were already ingenious; the seventh route connected locally earned discoveries.

GM Truth Ladder

  • After M01: someone besides Aletheia tracks redundancy.
  • After M02: the mark responds to failure recovery, not mere copying.
  • After M03: the Continuance protects people by preventing complete ownership.
  • After M04: the pattern predates modern intervention.
  • After M05: ancient refuge engineering uses the same grammar.
  • In M06: the team receives the complete distributed-building answer and chooses future custody.

Campaign End States

Open Continuance

The seventh route remains active under plural oversight. Corrections can travel backward, but no single authority selects them.

Covenant Severance

The route closes after the existing chain completes. Humanity inherits the Sanctuaries without further editing.

Aletheian Custody

Aletheia retains the route with transparent Vector oversight, maximizing survival while accepting the charge that it preserved its own rise.

Sevenfold Trust

Custody is divided among human, machine, archival, civic, scientific, local, and Vector stewards. It is least efficient and most resilient.

Safety and Historical Agency

M03 involves refugees, antisemitic persecution, borders, and state violence. Use lines and veils. Do not turn victims into puzzle furniture. Fiction supplies undocumented gaps around history; it does not steal credit from Varian Fry, colleagues, refugees, local helpers, mushers, engineers, divers, archaeologists, Nabataean builders, or prehistoric communities.
