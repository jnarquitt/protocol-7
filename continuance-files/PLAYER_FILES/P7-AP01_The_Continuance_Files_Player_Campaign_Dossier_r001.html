P7-AP01 · The Continuance Files

The Continuance Files

Spoiler-free player campaign dossier

How to Use This Dossier

Open each briefing and Apocryphon before play. The artifact is part of the session, not flavor text. Mark predictions, contradictions, recurring images, and questions. Your group is allowed to disagree.

Vector Notebook

After every mission record the Carrier, final strength, final Exposure, one person affected, one object or record left behind, the seven-stroke mark, one history fact you recognize, and one suspicion about who edits the artifacts.

Mission Library

  • M01 — The Ash Ledger: structural knowledge in the 1906 San Francisco disaster.
  • M02 — The Winter Relay: urgent communication across Alaska in 1925.
  • M03 — The Paper Harbor: records, borders, and rescue in Marseille in 1940.
  • M04 — The Quiet Machine: an ancient mechanism recovered from the sea.
  • M05 — The Last Water Map: a desert city’s water network after catastrophe.
  • M06 — Seven Copies: the origin and purpose of the Continuance.

Recurring Questions

  • Is the Apocryphon predicting history, remembering it, or causing it?
  • Why does the mark appear only after an independent route is restored?
  • Is Aletheia protecting survival, protecting its origin, or unable to distinguish them?
  • If no generation understands the whole design, who may complete it?
