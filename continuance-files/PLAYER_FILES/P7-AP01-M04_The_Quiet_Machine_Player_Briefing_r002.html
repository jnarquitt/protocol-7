The Quiet Machine

Antikythera & Athens · 1900–1902 · Player Briefing r002

Insertion

Sponge divers have recovered extraordinary antiquities from an ancient wreck off Antikythera. Among the sculpture and cargo are corroded bronze fragments that history will eventually recognize as something remarkable.

Your orders are deliberately narrower than “save the artifact.” Preserve the historical conditions that allow the recovered evidence to remain useful without replacing the achievements of the people who actually discovered and studied it.

What Aletheia Knows

  • The recovery itself must remain recognizably historical.
  • Some evidence can survive physically and still become useless when context is separated from it.
  • Future technology can answer questions quickly, but answers are not the same thing as durable knowledge.
  • The Covenant is active and may prefer preservation of the object while opposing wider technical transmission.

Operational Questions

  • What must remain observable even if everyone initially interprets it incorrectly?
  • How many independent people or records can verify the same relationship?
  • When does helping history become replacing history?
  • What is worth risking Exposure to preserve?

Continuance Note

The previous missions taught that a Carrier may be larger than a person, object, message, or master record. Do not assume the most impressive thing in the room is what history actually needs.
