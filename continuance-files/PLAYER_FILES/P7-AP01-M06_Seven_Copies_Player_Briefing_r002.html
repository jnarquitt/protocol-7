Protocol 7 · The Continuance Files · M06

Seven Copies

Player Briefing r002 · Level 6 finale

What You Know

Five Continuance missions have produced five different forms of survival: knowledge that can be reproduced, messages that can be reconstructed, identities that survive through independent records, evidence that can be checked again, and civic knowledge distributed among people rather than trapped in one map.

The seven-stroke mark has appeared across those repairs. It no longer behaves like a signature.

Insertion: Upper Mesopotamia, approximately 9600 BCE.
Known objective: Investigate the earliest verified appearance of the seven-stroke phenomenon.
Operational warning: Temporal reference at the destination is unstable.
Historical boundary: The people you encounter possess their own sophisticated knowledge, social networks, crafts, traditions, and solutions. You are not there to teach civilization to its ancestors.

Apocryphon

There are seven copies.

None contains the whole.

Six can find one another without you.

The seventh knows where you came from.

Before Insertion

Think about two questions for your Vector:

What has changed since San Francisco? Name one way your Vector is more capable, more certain, or less certain than at Level 1.

What would make you refuse Aletheia? Not distrust it. Refuse it.

Player Reminder

This is the finale of the first Continuance Files campaign. Prior mission choices may matter. There may not be a single optimal solution, and disagreement between Vectors is legitimate play so long as everyone at the table remains able to participate in the final decision.
