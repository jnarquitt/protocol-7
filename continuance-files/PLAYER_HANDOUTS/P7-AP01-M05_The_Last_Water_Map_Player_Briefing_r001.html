PROTOCOL 7 · THE CONTINUANCE FILES · M05

The Last Water Map

Player Briefing r001 · Petra · 363 CE

Mission Context

A major earthquake has struck Petra, an ancient city whose survival in an arid landscape depends upon an extensive water system developed, repaired and adapted over generations.

Aletheia has detected a discontinuity associated with the disaster. Evidence points toward a surviving record of the city's water network. Your initial assignment is to locate the record, determine what it preserves, and prevent the required Historical Carrier from disappearing.

The city remembers water in stone.

Seven keep the paths. Six point down. One points where water does not go.

When the mountain moves, the map becomes a lie.

Ask the people carrying empty jars.

What You Know

Environment: earthquake-damaged urban terrain, rock-cut passages, cisterns, channels, reservoirs and unstable structures.

Primary concern: access to water may become an immediate human emergency.

Temporal concern: obvious future technology could solve local problems quickly while producing severe historical Exposure.

Historical boundary: Petra's water engineering predates your arrival by centuries. You are not being sent to invent it.

Before Insertion

Decide what role your Vector can plausibly play around disaster response, infrastructure, logistics, medicine, negotiation, security or physical rescue.

Then answer one question: If saving an entire district required exposing something unmistakably from the future, how long would your Vector hesitate?
